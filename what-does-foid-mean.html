Slop Dictionary / foid

What Does Foid Mean?

Foid is what happens when the internet decides the word woman is apparently not weird enough.

Short version

Foid meaning in slang: foid is a derogatory internet term for a woman. It is shortened from femoid, a word associated with incel communities and built to make women sound less human.

Where does foid come from?

Foid is clipped from femoid. The Anti-Defamation League describes femoid as a blend of female with humanoid or android, used in incel spaces to reduce women to a subhuman category.

That is the whole grim little etymology. There is no secret second meaning where it turns out to be a neutral biology term.

How is it used now?

You will still see foid used sincerely in misogynistic corners of the internet. You will also see it used ironically, quoted, mocked, or folded into newer terms like foidslop.

That wider ironic use does not erase where the word came from. It does explain why someone can encounter it in a meme without having spent six years posting on an incel forum.

Foid vs femoid

Same concept, fewer letters. Femoid is the longer form. Foid is the version that survived because internet slang naturally optimizes toward typing less and sounding worse.

Why is this site called foidslop?

Because the compound word is memorable, funny, and useful in a way its source material was not. The site takes the insult, attaches it to ordinary things people actually enjoy, and refuses to act embarrassed about the result.

Related cursed vocabulary

foidslopWhat Is Foidslop?

Foidslop is internet slang for food, media, usernames, aesthetics, or other things perceived as strongly female-coded. The word started as an insult. It is also increasingly used as a joke, a label, and a reclaimed category by people who enjoy the exact things it is supposed to mock.

femoidWhat Does Femoid Mean?

Femoid is derogatory internet slang for a woman. It combines female with humanoid or android and is the longer form from which foid was clipped.

moidWhat Does Moid Mean?

Moid is derogatory or joking internet slang for a man. It was formed as a parallel to foid, with the same robotic-sounding construction applied in the other direction.

femcelWhat Does Femcel Mean?

Femcel originally means a woman who identifies with involuntary celibacy. Online, the term has spread into memes, aesthetics, self-deprecation, and broader jokes about female loneliness or social failure.
