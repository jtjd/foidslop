SILKY EGG
DROP SOUP.

Better than takeout. Silky egg ribbons in a gingery, savory broth.

Silky Egg Drop Soup is a small pot, so every minute changes the texture. Build the broth in stages, stop before good chicken broth loses its shape, and serve it hot.

At a Glance

Prep
3m
Cook
8m
Serves
1
Difficulty

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Build broth

    Simmer 1.5 cups of broth with 1 tsp ginger, 1 tsp soy, and 1 tsp sesame oil. Add 1 tsp cornstarch mixture to thicken.

  2. 02

    Add eggs

    Slowly pour 2 beaten eggs in a thin stream while stirring gently to form ribbons.

  3. 03

    Serve

    Top with spring onions, white pepper, and sesame seeds.

Easy swaps

Low-sodium chicken stock or vegetable broth both work. Use tamari instead of soy sauce for a gluten-free bowl, and arrowroot starch can replace cornstarch in the same amount.

Storage

Cool this soup promptly and keep it covered for up to three days. Warm one portion slowly and finish with fresh ginger so the fresh flavor survives.

Slop Notes

Pour the eggs in very slowly and stir very gently. Fast movement creates bits rather than ribbons.
