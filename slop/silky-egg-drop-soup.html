Slop of the Day — July 24, 2026

SILKY
EGG DROP SOUP.

Published July 24, 2026
Soup Quick Chinese
Silky Egg Drop Soup — foidslop

Better than takeout. Silky egg ribbons in a gingery, savory broth.

At a Glance

Prep
3m
Cook
8m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • good chicken broth 1.5 cups
  • fresh ginger, grated 1 tsp
  • soy sauce 1 tsp
  • sesame oil 1 tsp
  • 1 tbsp water 1 tsp cornstarch mixed with
  • eggs, beaten 2 large
  • spring onion, white pepper, sesame seeds to garnish

Method

  1. 01

    Build broth

    Simmer 1.5 cups of broth with 1 tsp ginger, 1 tsp soy, and 1 tsp sesame oil. Add 1 tsp cornstarch mixture to thicken.

  2. 02

    Add eggs

    Slowly pour 2 beaten eggs in a thin stream while stirring gently to form ribbons.

  3. 03

    Serve

    Top with spring onions, white pepper, and sesame seeds.

Slop Notes

Pour the eggs in very slowly and stir very gently. Fast movement creates bits rather than ribbons.
