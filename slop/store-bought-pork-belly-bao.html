Store-Bought Pork Belly Bao / foidslop
Home/Archive/Store-Bought Pork Belly Bao

Using supermarket char siu pork belly and frozen steamed buns. Restaurant at home.

The recipe keeps the portion practical without making dinner feel slight. Give the main ingredient enough time to brown or soften, then finish with contrast.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
10m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Steam buns

    Steam the frozen bao according to the package directions until puffed, soft, and hot through. Keep them covered off the heat so the surfaces do not dry out.

  2. 02

    Warm pork

    Warm 100g of pork belly slices in a hot pan until edges caramelize.

  3. 03

    Build

    Open bao, spread 2 tbsp hoisin inside, add pork, pickled vegetables, cilantro, and 1 tsp sriracha.

Slop Notes

Frozen bao buns are available at most Asian supermarkets and steam back to perfect in minutes.
