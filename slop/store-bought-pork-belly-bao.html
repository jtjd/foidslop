Using supermarket char siu pork belly and frozen steamed buns. Restaurant at home.

A short ingredient list lets Store-Bought Pork Belly Bao depend on texture. Season frozen steamed bao buns in stages, cook until the center is ready, and finish with something fresh or sharp.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Steam buns

    Steam the frozen bao according to the package directions until puffed, soft, and hot through. Keep them covered off the heat so the surfaces do not dry out.

  2. 02

    Warm pork

    Warm 100g of pork belly slices in a hot pan until edges caramelize.

  3. 03

    Build

    Open bao, spread 2 tbsp hoisin inside, add pork, pickled vegetables, cilantro, and 1 tsp sriracha.

Easy swaps

Small flour tortillas or lettuce cups can replace steamed bao buns. Char siu, roast pork, shredded chicken, or crisp tofu all work with hoisin and pickled vegetables.

Storage

Save leftovers in a sealed container for up to three days. Reheat slowly and finish with frozen steamed bao buns at the table rather than cooking the fresh element twice.

Slop Notes

Frozen bao buns are available at most Asian supermarkets and steam back to perfect in minutes.
