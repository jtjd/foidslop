SALMON AND AVOCADO TOAST.

Published September 30, 2026
Toast Salmon Quick
Smoked Salmon and Avocado Toast — foidslop
Home / Archive / Smoked Salmon and Avocado Toast

Creamy avocado and salty smoked salmon on crusty sourdough.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
3m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • sourdough, toasted 2 slices
  • ripe avocado 1/2
  • smoked salmon 60g
  • lemon juice squeeze
  • everything bagel seasoning 1 tsp
  • capers, fresh dill optional

Method

  1. 01

    Mash

    Mash the avocado onto the toasted sourdough. Squeeze with lemon juice.

  2. 02

    Top

    Drape the smoked salmon over the avocado. Sprinkle with everything bagel seasoning. Add capers and dill if using.

Slop Notes

The everything bagel seasoning ties the avocado and salmon together perfectly.
