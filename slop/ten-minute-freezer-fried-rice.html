10-MINUTE FREEZER
FRIED RICE.

Using entirely freezer ingredients. Frozen rice, vegetables, egg. Dinner in 10 minutes flat.

Use the pan as a tool for contrast in 10-Minute Freezer Fried Rice: hot crisp grains, tender frozen mixed vegetables, and a fresh finish from eggs added at the end.

At a Glance

Prep
2m
Cook
10m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Fry everything

    Fry 1 pouch of rice and 1/2 cup of frozen vegetables in 1 tbsp hot oil until crispy. Push aside, scramble 2 eggs, and fold through.

  2. 02

    Season

    Add 2 tsp soy sauce, toss, and finish with 1 tsp sesame oil and chilli crisp.

Easy swaps

Brown rice, quinoa, or cauliflower rice all work. Brown the base properly before adding frozen mixed vegetables; that toastiness is the whole trick.

Storage

Cool leftovers quickly, cover them, and keep them for up to three days. A hot pan restores the rice; add eggs only once the grains are warmed.

Slop Notes

Microwave pouches of rice work surprisingly well for fried rice because they are already slightly dried.
