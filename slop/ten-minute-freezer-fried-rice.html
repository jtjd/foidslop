Slop of the Day — July 18, 2026

10-MINUTE
FREEZER FRIED RICE.

Published July 18, 2026
Quick Pantry Rice
10-Minute Freezer Fried Rice — foidslop

Using entirely freezer ingredients. Frozen rice, vegetables, egg. Dinner in 10 minutes flat.

At a Glance

Prep
2m
Cook
10m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • microwave or frozen cooked rice 1 pouch
  • frozen mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, corn, edamame) 1/2 cup
  • eggs 2 large
  • soy sauce 2 tsp
  • sesame oil 1 tsp
  • neutral oil 1 tbsp
  • chilli crisp, sriracha, spring onion to taste

Method

  1. 01

    Fry everything

    Fry 1 pouch of rice and 1/2 cup of frozen vegetables in 1 tbsp hot oil until crispy. Push aside, scramble 2 eggs, and fold through.

  2. 02

    Season

    Add 2 tsp soy sauce, toss, and finish with 1 tsp sesame oil and chilli crisp.

Slop Notes

Microwave pouches of rice work surprisingly well for fried rice because they are already slightly dried.
