Italian store bought excellence arranged beautifully. Artichoke hearts, salami, olives, marinated things.

Treat Antipasto Plate like a small composed meal. Put salami or capicola at the center, use marinated artichoke hearts to keep the plate lively, and add the sharp or salty finish just before eating.

Method

  1. 01

    Arrange

    Lay out the 30g of salami, marinated artichoke hearts, olives, and mozzarella balls on a plate. Add the roasted red peppers and drizzle everything lightly with olive oil. Tear off pieces of focaccia or ciabatta to eat alongside.

Easy swaps

Swap salami or capicola for whatever is already open in the fridge, and let marinated artichoke hearts keep its original contrast. The formula matters more than the parts: substance, crunch, and something sharp to cut through.

Storage

The assembled plate loses its contrast in the fridge. Chill salami or capicola in a covered container, keep marinated artichoke hearts sealed on its own, and rebuild the portions at serving time.

Slop Notes

Castelvetrano olives are buttery and mild. Perfect for those who do not love super briny olives.
