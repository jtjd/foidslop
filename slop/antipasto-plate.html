Home/Archive/Antipasto Plate

Italian store bought excellence arranged beautifully. Artichoke hearts, salami, olives, marinated things.

This plate is built around contrast: creamy, crisp, salty, and fresh in every few bites. Give each ingredient its own space and assemble combinations as you eat.

Method

  1. 01

    Arrange

    Lay out the 30g of salami, marinated artichoke hearts, olives, and mozzarella balls on a plate. Add the roasted red peppers and drizzle everything lightly with olive oil. Tear off pieces of focaccia or ciabatta to eat alongside.

Slop Notes

Castelvetrano olives are buttery and mild. Perfect for those who do not love super briny olives.
