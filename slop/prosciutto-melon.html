The Italian classic that needs no recipe. Sweet cantaloupe and salty prosciutto.

The arrangement is part of Prosciutto and Melon. Leave ripe cantaloupe melon and prosciutto di Parma in their own spaces, then let the creamy, crisp, and briny parts meet in different combinations.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Plate

    Lay the 1/4 sliced ripe cantaloupe on a plate. Drape the 3 to 4 slices of prosciutto over each slice. Drizzle with olive oil, add black pepper and flaky salt. Add fresh mint or arugula if using.

Easy swaps

No ripe cantaloupe melon? Any cheese, hummus, or tinned fish can anchor the plate. Keep one creamy thing, one crisp thing, and one briny thing and the board still works.

Storage

The assembled plate loses its contrast in the fridge. Chill ripe cantaloupe melon in a covered container, keep prosciutto di Parma sealed on its own, and rebuild the portions at serving time.

Slop Notes

Only works with truly ripe, fragrant melon. If the melon is not sweet, this dish is pointless.
