AVOCADO
AND EGG TOAST.

Published October 26, 2026
Toast Eggs Quick
Avocado and Egg Toast — foidslop
Home / Archive / Avocado and Egg Toast

Mashed avocado on toast, topped with a fried or poached egg.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
5m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • sourdough, toasted 2 slices
  • ripe avocado 1/2
  • eggs 2
  • butter or oil 1 tbsp
  • lemon juice squeeze
  • flaky salt, chilli flakes to taste

Method

  1. 01

    Mash

    Mash the avocado onto the toast. Squeeze with lemon juice.

  2. 02

    Fry eggs

    Heat butter in a pan. Fry the eggs until the whites are set and the yolks are runny.

  3. 03

    Top

    Place the eggs on top of the avocado toast. Sprinkle with flaky salt and chilli flakes.

Slop Notes

The runny yolk mixing with the avocado is the best part.
