MUSHROOM
AND THYME OMELETTE.

Published November 4, 2026
Eggs Quick Comfort
Mushroom and Thyme Omelette — foidslop
Home / Archive / Mushroom and Thyme Omelette

A fluffy folded omelette filled with savory mushrooms and thyme.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
10m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Mid

Ingredients

  • eggs 3 large
  • mushrooms, sliced 1 cup
  • fresh thyme leaves 1 tsp
  • butter 1 tbsp
  • gruyere or swiss cheese, shredded 30g
  • salt, black pepper to taste

Method

  1. 01

    Cook mushrooms

    Melt half the butter in a pan. Add mushrooms and thyme. Cook until browned. Set aside.

  2. 02

    Make omelette

    Whisk the eggs with salt and pepper. Melt the rest of the butter in the pan. Pour in the eggs. When the edges set, add the mushrooms and cheese. Fold over and serve.

Slop Notes

Cook the omelette over medium-low heat for a soft, creamy texture.
