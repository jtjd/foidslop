Warm, savory, deeply comforting. A proper miso broth with noodles and soft tofu.

Cook the noodles separately so they do not cloud or over-thicken the broth. Warm the tofu gently, take the broth off the heat, and dissolve the miso in a ladleful of hot liquid before stirring it back into the pot.

Method

  1. 01

    Make broth

    Bring 2 cups of water or dashi to a gentle simmer. Add the cubed tofu and warm for 2 minutes, then remove the pan from the heat. Mix 2 tablespoons of miso with a ladleful of hot broth in a small bowl until smooth, then stir it back into the pan with the soy sauce and sesame oil.

  2. 02

    Add and serve

    Cook 1 portion of noodles separately according to the package, drain well, and place in a bowl. Ladle over the miso broth and tofu, then finish with spring onions, nori, and sesame seeds.

Easy swaps

Use red or yellow miso for a stronger broth, starting with one tablespoon and adding more to taste. Firm tofu can replace silken tofu, and soba, somen, or instant ramen noodles all work. Use tamari instead of soy sauce for a gluten-free version.

Storage

Store broth, noodles, and tofu separately when possible for up to two days. Reheat the broth gently without boiling, then add the noodles and tofu just long enough to warm through.

Slop Notes

Miso loses aroma when boiled. Remove the pan from the heat before adding it, and taste before adding soy sauce because both ingredients contribute salt.
