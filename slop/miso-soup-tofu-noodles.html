Miso Soup with Tofu and Noodles / foidslop
Home/Archive/Miso Soup with Tofu and Noodles

Warm, savory, deeply comforting. A proper miso broth with noodles and soft tofu.

The pot is small enough that a few minutes make a real difference. Simmer until the flavors come together, then stop before the vegetables or noodles lose their shape.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
10m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Make broth

    Heat 2 cups of water to just below boiling. Whisk in 2 tablespoons of miso paste until dissolved. Add 1 teaspoon of soy sauce and 1 teaspoon of sesame oil.

  2. 02

    Add and serve

    Cook 1 portion of noodles separately and add to a bowl. Ladle the hot broth over, add 100g of cubed silken tofu, spring onions, nori, and sesame seeds.

Slop Notes

Never boil miso. Add it at the very end off the heat for best flavor.
