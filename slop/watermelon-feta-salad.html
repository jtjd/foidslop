Juicy watermelon, salty feta, fresh mint, and lime make a bright salad with a clean sweet-savory contrast.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Prepare

    In a bowl, gently toss the watermelon cubes with feta and mint.

  2. 02

    Dress and finish

    Drizzle with olive oil and lime juice. Season with flaky salt and pepper.

Slop Notes

Do not toss too hard or the watermelon will break down and get mushy.
