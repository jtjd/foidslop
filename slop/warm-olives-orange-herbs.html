Home/Archive/Warm Olives with Orange and Herbs

Olives gently warmed in olive oil with citrus and herbs. A 5 minute pot of wonder.

A small snack can still deserve a real plate and a little attention. Keep the crisp things crisp, the warm things warm, and do not be shy with seasoning.

At a Glance

Prep
3m
Cook
5m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Warm

    Combine 1 cup of olives, 3 tablespoons of olive oil, 2 strips of orange peel, 1 sprig of rosemary, 1 clove of garlic, and chilli flakes in a pan. Warm gently over low heat for 5 minutes.

  2. 02

    Serve

    Pour into a bowl with all the oil. Serve with crusty bread for dipping in the oil.

Slop Notes

The flavored oil at the bottom is the best part. Make sure you have bread for it.
