LABNEH WITH ZA'ATAR
AND OLIVE OIL.

Strained yogurt cheese with za'atar and olive oil. The Middle Eastern answer to cream cheese.

Use a shallow bowl for Labneh with Za'atar and Olive Oil so labneh or thick Greek yogurt and the finishing oil or herbs have room to show up in every scoop. Taste before adding more salt.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Plate

    Spread 1/2 cup of labneh in a wide bowl. Drizzle with 2 tablespoons of olive oil and dust heavily with 1 tablespoon of za'atar. Serve with warm pita, radishes, and cucumber.

Easy swaps

No labneh or thick Greek yogurt? White beans, roasted eggplant, or extra yogurt blend into an equally dippable base. Taste and re-salt after blending.

Storage

Refrigerate the labneh mixture in a covered container for up to three days. Keep pita and vegetables separate, then add fresh olive oil and za'atar after the labneh has lost some of its refrigerator chill.

Slop Notes

Za'atar is a blend of dried thyme, sesame seeds, sumac, and salt. Find it at any Middle Eastern grocery.
