LABNEH WITH ZA'ATAR
AND OLIVE OIL.

Published July 7, 2026

Labneh with Za'atar and Olive Oil — foidslop
Home/Archive/Labneh with Za'atar and Olive Oil

Strained yogurt cheese with za'atar and olive oil. The Middle Eastern answer to cream cheese.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • labneh or thick Greek yogurt1/2 cup
  • za'atar spice blend1 tbsp
  • good olive oil2 tbsp
  • warm pita, radishes, cucumber, olivesto serve
  • sliced tomatoes, mintoptional

Method

  1. 01

    Plate

    Spread 1/2 cup of labneh in a wide bowl. Drizzle with 2 tablespoons of olive oil and dust heavily with 1 tablespoon of za'atar. Serve with warm pita, radishes, and cucumber.

Slop Notes

Za'atar is a blend of dried thyme, sesame seeds, sumac, and salt. Find it at any Middle Eastern grocery.
