Home/Archive/Tahini and Honey Toast

Nutty tahini with honey and sesame seeds. A two ingredient toast that is weirdly addictive.

The bread needs to be deeply crisp before the topping goes on. That sturdy base keeps the last bite as good as the first and gives the softer ingredients some contrast.

At a Glance

Prep
2m
Cook
3m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Toast

    Toast the bread until deeply golden, crisp at the edges, and still a little tender in the center. Move it to a plate while it is hot.

  2. 02

    Top

    Spread 2 tbsp of tahini generously while bread is hot. Drizzle 1 tbsp honey over the top. Finish with a pinch of flaky salt and sesame seeds.

Slop Notes

Good tahini is essential. It should be pourable and smell nutty. If it tastes bitter it is a bad batch.
