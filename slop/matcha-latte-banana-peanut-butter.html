The girl dinner that is just a drink and a banana with nut butter. Deeply valid.

A small snack still deserves attention. Let ceremonial grade matcha powder carry the bite, use oat milk for contrast, and add the salt, heat, or acid while the food is at its best.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Make matcha

    Sift 1 teaspoon of matcha into a mug, add 2 oz of hot water, and whisk vigorously until frothy. Pour in 6 oz of warm frothed oat milk.

  2. 02

    Add oat milk

    Serve with 1 banana and 2 tablespoons of peanut butter for dipping.

Easy swaps

Missing ceremonial grade matcha powder? Any sturdy cracker-worthy substitute works. The point is contrast between bites, not exact replication.

Storage

For leftovers, cool any cooked parts promptly and store them away from the crisp finish. Reheat ceremonial grade matcha powder gently and add oat milk only at the table.

Slop Notes

Ceremonial grade matcha is not a luxury. It is the only kind that tastes good when made simply.
