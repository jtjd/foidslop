Slop of the Day — July 1, 2026

MATCHA
LATTE AND BANANA WITH PEANUT BUTTER.

Published July 1, 2026
Drink Sweet No Cook
Matcha Latte and Banana with Peanut Butter — foidslop

The girl dinner that is just a drink and a banana with nut butter. Deeply valid.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • ceremonial grade matcha powder 1 tsp
  • hot water (175F/80C, not boiling) 2 oz
  • oat milk, frothed or heated 6 oz
  • honey or maple syrup (optional) 1 tsp
  • banana 1
  • peanut or almond butter for dipping 2 tbsp

Method

  1. 01

    Make matcha

    Sift 1 teaspoon of matcha into a mug, add 2 oz of hot water, and whisk vigorously until frothy. Pour in 6 oz of warm frothed oat milk.

  2. 02

    Add oat milk

    Serve with 1 banana and 2 tablespoons of peanut butter for dipping.

Slop Notes

Ceremonial grade matcha is not a luxury. It is the only kind that tastes good when made simply.
