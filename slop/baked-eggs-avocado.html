BAKED EGGS
IN AVOCADO.

Published August 23, 2026

Baked Eggs in Avocado / foidslop
Home/Archive/Baked Eggs in Avocado

Eggs baked inside avocado halves make a rich breakfast-for-dinner with crisp edges and a soft center.

The difference between tender eggs and dry ones is usually the final minute. Watch the texture rather than the clock and serve as soon as they are set.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
15m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Prep

    Preheat oven to 425F/220C. Halve the avocado and remove the pit. Scoop out a little extra flesh to make the hole bigger. Place the avocado halves in a baking dish to keep them stable.

  2. 02

    Bake

    Crack an egg into each avocado hole. Season with salt, pepper, and chilli flakes. Bake for 15 minutes until the egg whites are set.

Slop Notes

Use small eggs or the whites will overflow. You can also crack the egg into a cup first and pour it in.
