BAKED EGGS
IN AVOCADO.

Eggs baked inside avocado halves make a rich breakfast-for-dinner with crisp edges and a soft center.

Baked Eggs in Avocado is simple food with no room to hide overcooking. Keep the heat controlled, let large avocado warm through, and stop when the center still looks a little loose.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Prep

    Preheat oven to 425F/220C. Halve the avocado and remove the pit. Scoop out a little extra flesh to make the hole bigger. Place the avocado halves in a baking dish to keep them stable.

  2. 02

    Bake

    Crack an egg into each avocado hole. Season with salt, pepper, and chilli flakes. Bake for 15 minutes until the egg whites are set.

Easy swaps

Out of small eggs? Whatever needs using up works instead. Keep the method identical and season in layers so the eggs stay the main event.

Storage

If saving a portion, refrigerate it covered for one day. Reheat slowly so the eggs stay tender, then add small eggs at the end for contrast.

Slop Notes

Use small eggs or the whites will overflow. You can also crack the egg into a cup first and pour it in.
