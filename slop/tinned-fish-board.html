The elevated pantry dinner. Sardines, smoked oysters, arranged on a board with all the accompaniments.

There is no cooking trick hiding here. The pleasure comes from choosing good ingredients, arranging enough for a real meal, and changing the bite as you go.

At a Glance

Prep
10m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Open and arrange

    Open the 1 tin of sardines and 1 tin of smoked oysters and place them directly on a board. Arrange crackers, 2 tablespoons of creme fraiche, 1 tablespoon of dijon mustard, cornichons, and capers around them. Add a lemon wedge.

Slop Notes

The open tins are part of the aesthetic. Place them directly on the board. Fishwife and Ortiz are the top brands.
