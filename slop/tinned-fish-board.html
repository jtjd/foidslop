Slop of the Day — June 19, 2026

TINNED
FISH BOARD.

Published June 19, 2026
Tinned Fish No Cook Fancy
Tinned Fish Board — foidslop

The elevated pantry dinner. Sardines, smoked oysters, arranged on a board with all the accompaniments.

At a Glance

Prep
10m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • sardines in olive oil 1 tin
  • smoked oysters or mussels 1 tin
  • Ritz crackers or rye crispbreads handful
  • creme fraiche or cream cheese 2 tbsp
  • dijon mustard 1 tbsp
  • cornichons and capers handful
  • lemon 1/4
  • hot sauce, sliced radishes optional

Method

  1. 01

    Open and arrange

    Open the 1 tin of sardines and 1 tin of smoked oysters and place them directly on a board. Arrange crackers, 2 tablespoons of creme fraiche, 1 tablespoon of dijon mustard, cornichons, and capers around them. Add a lemon wedge.

Slop Notes

The open tins are part of the aesthetic. Place them directly on the board. Fishwife and Ortiz are the top brands.
