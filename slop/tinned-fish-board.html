The elevated pantry dinner. Sardines, smoked oysters, arranged on a board with all the accompaniments.

The arrangement is part of Tinned Fish Board. Leave sardines and smoked oysters or mussels in their own spaces, then let the creamy, crisp, and briny parts meet in different combinations.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Open and arrange

    Open the 1 tin of sardines and 1 tin of smoked oysters and place them directly on a board. Arrange crackers, 2 tablespoons of creme fraiche, 1 tablespoon of dijon mustard, cornichons, and capers around them. Add a lemon wedge.

Easy swaps

No sardines? Any cheese, hummus, or tinned fish can anchor the plate. Keep one creamy thing, one crisp thing, and one briny thing and the board still works.

Storage

The assembled plate loses its contrast in the fridge. Chill sardines in a covered container, keep smoked oysters or mussels sealed on its own, and rebuild the portions at serving time.

Slop Notes

The open tins are part of the aesthetic. Place them directly on the board. Fishwife and Ortiz are the top brands.
