Spicy Vodka Pasta — foidslop
Home / Archive / Spicy Vodka Pasta

The viral Gigi Hadid pasta. Creamy, spicy, and ready in 20 minutes.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
20m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • pasta (penne or rigatoni) 100g
  • butter 2 tbsp
  • tomato paste 2 tbsp
  • vodka (optional) 1 tbsp
  • heavy cream 1/4 cup
  • chilli flakes 1 tsp
  • parmesan, grated 30g
  • salt, black pepper to taste

Method

  1. 01

    Make sauce

    Melt butter in a pan. Add tomato paste and chilli flakes, cooking for 2 minutes. Deglaze with vodka. Stir in heavy cream and parmesan until smooth.

  2. 02

    Cook pasta

    Boil pasta in salted water until al dente. Reserve a splash of pasta water.

  3. 03

    Combine

    Toss the drained pasta in the sauce, adding pasta water to loosen if needed. Season and serve.

Slop Notes

The vodka is optional but it helps release flavors in the tomato paste that are alcohol soluble.
