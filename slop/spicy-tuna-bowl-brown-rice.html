WITH BROWN RICE.

Sushi-grade tuna tossed with spicy mayo, soy sauce, and sesame oil makes a bold topping for warm brown rice.

The finish matters in Spicy Tuna Bowl with Brown Rice. Keep the creamy, crisp, and savory parts separate long enough to notice them, then bring them together one bite at a time.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Season tuna

    In a bowl, toss the cubed tuna with soy sauce, sriracha mayo, and sesame oil.

  2. 02

    Assemble

    Place the warm brown rice in a bowl. Top with the spicy tuna. Garnish with sesame seeds, spring onions, and avocado slices.

Easy swaps

Rice, farro, couscous, or frozen grains all make the base. No sushi grade tuna? Any roasted vegetable or canned bean drops straight in.

Storage

Cool cooked components promptly and refrigerate for three days. Bring the base back with gentle heat, then add sushi grade tuna and any crunch at the table.

Slop Notes

Brown rice adds a nutty flavor and extra fiber. Make sure it is still warm when you assemble the bowl.
