SWEET
POTATO TOAST WITH ALMOND BUTTER.

Published September 26, 2026
Healthy Sweet Low Carb
Sweet Potato Toast with Almond Butter — foidslop
Home / Archive / Sweet Potato Toast with Almond Butter

Slices of sweet potato toasted and topped with almond butter and banana.

At a Glance

Prep
2m
Cook
10m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • sweet potato 1
  • almond butter 2 tbsp
  • banana, sliced 1/2
  • honey 1 tsp
  • cinnamon pinch

Method

  1. 01

    Toast sweet potato

    Slice the sweet potato lengthwise into 1/4 inch thick slices. Toast in a toaster on high setting twice, or until tender.

  2. 02

    Top

    Spread almond butter on the sweet potato toasts. Top with banana slices, a drizzle of honey, and a sprinkle of cinnamon.

Slop Notes

This is a great gluten free alternative to traditional toast.
