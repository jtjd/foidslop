Hummus and Crispy Chickpea Bowl / foidslop
Home/Archive/Hummus and Crispy Chickpea Bowl

Creamy hummus topped with crunchy, spiced roasted chickpeas for textural contrast.

Build the bowl in layers instead of stirring everything into one texture. A seasoned base, a substantial topping, and something bright or crunchy make each bite a little different.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
25m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Roast chickpeas

    Toss the dried chickpeas with olive oil, cumin, paprika, and salt. Roast at 400F/200C for 20-25 minutes until crispy.

  2. 02

    Assemble

    Spoon the hummus into a bowl. Top with the warm crispy chickpeas. Drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with parsley.

Slop Notes

Make sure the chickpeas are completely dry before roasting so they crisp up instead of steaming.
