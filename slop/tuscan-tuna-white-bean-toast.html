Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Make topping

    In a bowl, combine tuna, beans, red onion, vinegar, olive oil, and parsley. Season with salt and pepper.

  2. 02

    Toast

    Toast the bread until golden. Spoon the tuna mixture generously over the top.

Slop Notes

Using tuna packed in olive oil is essential for the authentic Tuscan flavor.
