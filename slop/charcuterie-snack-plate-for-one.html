All the hits of a charcuterie board scaled down to a solo dinner plate.

Treat Charcuterie Snack Plate for One like a small composed meal. Put salami or prosciutto at the center, use aged cheddar or manchego to keep the plate lively, and add the sharp or salty finish just before eating.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Arrange

    Fan the 30g of salami or prosciutto out on one side of a plate. Place the 30g of sliced or cubed aged cheddar on the opposite side. Fill the middle with a handful of crackers. Add a small bowl with 1 tablespoon of mustard, and scatter handfuls of grapes, olives, and cornichons in the gaps so everything is easy to reach. Leave some white space on the plate so it does not look crowded.

Easy swaps

Swap salami or prosciutto for whatever is already open in the fridge, and let aged cheddar or manchego keep its original contrast. The formula matters more than the parts: substance, crunch, and something sharp to cut through.

Storage

Store the pieces of this plate separately for up to two days. Cover salami or prosciutto, protect aged cheddar or manchego from moisture, and bring everything together only when you are ready to eat.

Slop Notes

Odd numbers look better. Three types of element minimum. Do not crowd. Leave some white space on the plate.
