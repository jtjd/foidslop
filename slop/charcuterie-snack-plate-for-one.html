Slop of the Day — May 13, 2026

CHARCUTERIE
SNACK PLATE FOR ONE.

Published May 13, 2026
No Cook Classic Cheese
Charcuterie Snack Plate for One — foidslop

All the hits of a charcuterie board scaled down to a solo dinner plate.

At a Glance

Prep
8m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • salami or prosciutto, sliced 30g
  • aged cheddar or manchego, sliced or cubed 30g
  • crackers (variety if possible) handful
  • whole grain or dijon mustard 1 tbsp
  • grapes or sliced apple handful
  • olives handful
  • cornichons, honeycomb, nuts optional

Method

  1. 01

    Arrange

    Fan the 30g of salami or prosciutto out on one side of a plate. Place the 30g of sliced or cubed aged cheddar on the opposite side. Fill the middle with a handful of crackers. Add a small bowl with 1 tablespoon of mustard, and scatter handfuls of grapes, olives, and cornichons in the gaps so everything is easy to reach. Leave some white space on the plate so it does not look crowded.

Slop Notes

Odd numbers look better. Three types of element minimum. Do not crowd. Leave some white space on the plate.
