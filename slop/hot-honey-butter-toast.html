HOT HONEY
BUTTER TOAST.

The most minimal toast possible. Just good bread, real butter, and hot honey.

Hot Honey Butter Toast depends on a properly crisp base. Toast the bread until it can support salted butter, add hot honey at the last minute, and eat it before the topping softens the edges.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Toast and butter

    Toast 2 slices of bread until golden. Spread 2 tablespoons of butter generously while still hot so it melts in.

  2. 02

    Drizzle

    Drizzle 1 tablespoon of hot honey over the buttery toast and add a pinch of flaky salt.

Easy swaps

No salted butter? Soft cheeses, mashed avocado, or ricotta play the same role, while hot honey can keep the topping interesting. Whatever the spread, season it directly instead of salting only the top.

Storage

Make the components ahead, not the finished toast. Cover salted butter, protect hot honey from moisture, and assemble after the bread is hot and crisp.

Slop Notes

This sounds almost too simple to be a meal, but the contrast works. Use thick bread and let it get properly crisp before adding the butter.
