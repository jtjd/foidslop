HOT HONEY
BUTTER TOAST.

Published June 25, 2026
ToastSweet-SavoryQuick
Hot Honey Butter Toast / foidslop
Home/Archive/Hot Honey Butter Toast

The most minimal toast possible. Just good bread, real butter, and hot honey.

A thick slice of bread turns a few strong ingredients into dinner. The useful details are browning, enough seasoning, and eating it while the toast still crackles.

At a Glance

Prep
2m
Cook
3m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Toast and butter

    Toast 2 slices of bread until golden. Spread 2 tablespoons of butter generously while still hot so it melts in.

  2. 02

    Drizzle

    Drizzle 1 tablespoon of hot honey over the buttery toast and add a pinch of flaky salt.

Slop Notes

This sounds almost too simple to be a meal, but the contrast works. Use thick bread and let it get properly crisp before adding the butter.
