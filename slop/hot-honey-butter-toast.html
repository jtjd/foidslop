HOT HONEY
BUTTER TOAST.

Published June 25, 2026

Hot Honey Butter Toast — foidslop
Home/Archive/Hot Honey Butter Toast

The most minimal toast possible. Just good bread, real butter, and hot honey.

At a Glance

Prep
2m
Cook
3m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Toast and butter

    Toast 2 slices of bread until golden. Spread 2 tablespoons of butter generously while still hot so it melts in.

  2. 02

    Drizzle

    Drizzle 1 tablespoon of hot honey over the buttery toast and add a pinch of flaky salt.

Slop Notes

This sounds too simple to be a meal. It is not.
