CRUNCHWRAP.

Flour tortilla folded around all the Taco Bell fixings. The ultimate comfort DIY.

A short ingredient list lets Homemade Crunchwrap depend on texture. Season sour cream in stages, cook until the center is ready, and finish with something fresh or sharp.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Layer

    Spread 2 tablespoons of sour cream, 1/4 cup of beef or beans, a handful of cheese, 1 tostada, a handful of lettuce, and 2 tablespoons of salsa in the center of a large tortilla.

  2. 02

    Fold

    Fold the edges up in pleats all the way around. Press flat.

  3. 03

    Toast

    Cook in a dry pan seam side down over medium heat, pressing down, until golden and sealed.

Easy swaps

Greek yogurt or Mexican crema can replace sour cream. Use seasoned black beans instead of beef, and swap the crisp tostada for a handful of sturdy tortilla chips if needed.

Storage

Cool the cooked portion promptly and keep it covered for three days. Warm it at moderate heat, then add seasoned ground beef or black beans after reheating so it keeps its texture.

Slop Notes

The tostada in the middle is the key structural element. It gives you that crunchy layer.
