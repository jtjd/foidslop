Only three ingredients but requires technique. Cheese, pepper, and pasta water do everything.

Use one pot and one generous appetite for Cacio e Pepe. Pull the pasta while it still has a little bite, then let Parmigiano-Reggiano and the starchy water finish the texture in the pan.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Toast pepper

    Toast 1.5 teaspoons of cracked pepper in a dry pan until fragrant. Boil 100g of pasta in salted water until 2 minutes before done, saving a cup of water.

  2. 02

    Make sauce

    Transfer pasta to the pepper pan over medium heat, adding pasta water gradually. Remove from heat, add 50g of pecorino and 20g of parmesan, and toss vigorously until emulsified into a silky sauce.

Easy swaps

Sauce components swap freely: if Pecorino Romano is out, any bold condiment thinned with pasta water (pesto, tapenade, chilli crisp) leads the bowl instead.

Storage

The sauce keeps covered for two days, while the pasta is best revived in a pan over low heat. Add Parmigiano-Reggiano after warming so its flavor stays clear.

Slop Notes

Taking the pan off heat before adding cheese prevents it from seizing. The pasta water emulsion is the entire technique.
