Spaghetti with garlic, olive oil, chilli, and parsley. Italian simplicity at its finest.

The sauce is simple enough to make while the water heats. Reserve a splash of starchy cooking water before draining because it is what helps the whole bowl come together.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Cook pasta

    Boil 100g of spaghetti in well salted water until al dente, saving a cup of water.

  2. 02

    Toast garlic

    Warm 3 tablespoons of olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add 4 cloves of sliced garlic and cook slowly until golden. Add 1/2 teaspoon of chilli flakes.

  3. 03

    Combine

    Add drained pasta and a splash of pasta water. Toss vigorously until emulsified. Add a large handful of parsley and toss again.

Easy swaps

Sauce components swap freely: if garlic is out, any bold condiment thinned with pasta water (pesto, tapenade, chilli crisp) leads the bowl instead.

Storage

Refrigerate up to two days. Reheat gently in a covered pan with a splash of water to bring the sauce back to glossy; microwaving works at half power.

Slop Notes

Garlic must be sliced and toasted slowly to golden. Never brown or it becomes bitter.
