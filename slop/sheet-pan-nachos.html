SHEET PAN
NACHOS.

Published June 23, 2026
ComfortSnackCheese
Sheet Pan Nachos — foidslop
Home/Archive/Sheet Pan Nachos

Maximum chip to topping ratio achieved by spreading everything on one tray.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
12m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Layer

    Spread 2 large handfuls of tortilla chips in a single layer on a baking sheet. Scatter 1/4 can of drained black beans, 2 tablespoons of pickled jalapenos, and 80g of shredded cheese over every chip.

  2. 02

    Bake

    Bake at 400F/200C for 8 to 12 minutes until melted.

  3. 03

    Top and eat

    Add cold sour cream and salsa. Eat directly from the tray.

Slop Notes

Eat straight from the sheet pan. It keeps them crispy and saves a dish.
