APPLE
AND CHEDDAR TOAST.

Published September 18, 2026
Toast Sweet-Savory Quick
Apple and Cheddar Toast — foidslop
Home / Archive / Apple and Cheddar Toast

Toasted bread with sharp cheddar and crisp apple slices. A Fall classic.

At a Glance

Prep
3m
Cook
3m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • sourdough, toasted 2 slices
  • sharp cheddar, sliced 40g
  • crisp apple, thinly sliced 1/2
  • honey drizzle
  • flaky sea salt pinch

Method

  1. 01

    Assemble

    Place cheddar slices on the toasted bread. Top with apple slices. Drizzle with honey and sprinkle with flaky salt.

  2. 02

    Melt

    Place under the broiler for 1-2 minutes until the cheese is just melted.

Slop Notes

The combination of apple and cheddar is a New England tradition for a reason.
