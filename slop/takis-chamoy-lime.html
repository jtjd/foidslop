Slop of the Day — July 27, 2026

TAKIS
WITH CHAMOY AND LIME.

Published July 27, 2026
Snack Spicy Viral
Takis with Chamoy and Lime — foidslop
Home / Archive / Takis with Chamoy and Lime

Hot chips drizzled with chamoy and lime. The chaotic snack dinner that TikTok loves.

At a Glance

Prep
3m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • Takis Fuego or Flamin Hot Cheetos 1 bag
  • chamoy sauce 2 tbsp
  • lime, juiced 1
  • Tajin seasoning pinch
  • cucumber sticks, mango slices, jicama optional

Method

  1. 01

    Plate

    Pour 1 bag of chips into a bowl. Drizzle with 2 tablespoons of chamoy, squeeze 1 lime over, and dust with Tajin. Add fresh fruit and vegetables on the side if using.

Slop Notes

Adding fruit alongside the chips makes it a significantly more balanced and interesting meal. Mango and cucumber are ideal.
