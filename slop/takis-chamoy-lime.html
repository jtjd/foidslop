Takis with fresh lime, chamoy, Tajin, cucumber, and mango for a spicy, sour five-minute snack plate.

Add lime and chamoy in two light passes, tossing between each, so the seasoning coats the Takis without pooling at the bottom. Serve cucumber, mango, or jicama alongside for a cool, crisp contrast.

Method

  1. 01

    Plate

    Pour 1 bag of chips into a wide bowl. Drizzle with half the chamoy and lime, toss gently, then repeat only if needed. Dust with Tajin and serve immediately with cucumber, mango, or jicama on the side.

Easy swaps

Flamin Hot Cheetos or sturdy tortilla chips can replace Takis. Use Valentina or another tangy hot sauce if chamoy is unavailable, and add extra mango or cucumber when you want less heat.

Storage

Dress only the amount you plan to eat because lime and chamoy soften the chips quickly. Keep unopened chips at room temperature and refrigerate cut fruit separately for up to one day.

Slop Notes

Start with half the chamoy and lime, toss, then taste. Takis are already salty and acidic, so a lighter hand keeps the chile and corn flavors recognizable.
