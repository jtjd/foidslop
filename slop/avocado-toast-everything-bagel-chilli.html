Avocado Toast with Everything Bagel and Chilli Flakes / foidslop
Home/Archive/Avocado Toast with Everything Bagel and Chilli Flakes

Yes it is basic. Yes it is still perfect. The seasoning and chilli flakes make it.

The bread needs to be deeply crisp before the topping goes on. That sturdy base keeps the last bite as good as the first and gives the softer ingredients some contrast.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
3m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Toast

    Toast the 2 slices of thick sourdough until deeply golden and crisp.

  2. 02

    Mash and top

    Halve the 1 large ripe avocado, remove the pit, and scoop the flesh directly onto the hot toast. Use a fork to mash it roughly right on the bread. Immediately squeeze fresh lemon or lime juice over the avocado to prevent browning. Shower with 1 teaspoon of everything bagel seasoning, a pinch of chilli flakes, and a sprinkle of flaky salt.

Slop Notes

A fried or poached egg makes the toast more substantial. Pomegranate seeds add a tart pop and extra crunch.
