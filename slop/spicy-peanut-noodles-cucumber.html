PEANUT NOODLES WITH CUCUMBER.

Published October 10, 2026

Spicy Peanut Noodles with Cucumber — foidslop
Home / Archive / Spicy Peanut Noodles with Cucumber

Cold noodles in a spicy peanut sauce with crisp cucumber ribbons.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
10m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • soba or ramen noodles 100g
  • peanut butter 2 tbsp
  • soy sauce 1 tbsp
  • rice vinegar 1 tbsp
  • sesame oil 1 tsp
  • sriracha 1 tsp
  • cucumber, shaved into ribbons 1/2
  • sesame seeds, spring onion to garnish

Method

  1. 01

    Cook noodles

    Boil noodles according to package. Drain and rinse under cold water.

  2. 02

    Make sauce

    Whisk peanut butter, soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and sriracha. Thin with warm water until pourable.

  3. 03

    Toss

    Toss the cold noodles with the sauce. Top with cucumber ribbons, sesame seeds, and spring onions.

Slop Notes

The crisp cucumber cools down the spicy peanut sauce perfectly.
