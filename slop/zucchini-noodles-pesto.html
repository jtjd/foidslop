WITH PESTO.

Tender zucchini noodles tossed with basil pesto and parmesan make a light, quick dinner for one.

Zucchini Noodles with Pesto comes together when the noodles finish in the sauce. Keep zucchini close, save some cooking water, and stop as soon as the bowl turns glossy.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Saute zucchini

    Heat olive oil in a pan. Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds. Add zucchini noodles and toss for 2-3 minutes until just wilted but still crunchy.

  2. 02

    Dress

    Remove from heat. Toss with pesto. Top with parmesan and any optional additions.

Easy swaps

Different short pasta shapes swap freely here. No zucchini? Pecorino, asiago, or extra-black-pepper parmesan covers the same role in the sauce.

Storage

Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate for up to two days. Warm them slowly with a little water, then finish with zucchini just before serving.

Slop Notes

Do not overcook the zucchini. Stop when the strands are just tender so they do not release too much water.
