Sweet peach, salty prosciutto, creamy burrata. Summer on a plate with no cooking required.

Think of this as dinner with the assembly left visible. Something rich, something briny, and something crisp keep the plate interesting without creating extra work.

Method

  1. 01

    Plate

    Place the 1 ball of fresh burrata in the center of a beautiful plate. Slice the 1 ripe peach and arrange the slices around the burrata. Drape the 3 slices of prosciutto di Parma over the peaches. Tuck a handful of fresh basil leaves into the gaps. Just before eating, tear the burrata open so the creamy center spills out. Finish with a drizzle of good olive oil, a swirl of balsamic glaze, and a pinch of flaky salt.

Easy swaps

Swap fresh burrata for whatever is already open in the fridge. The formula matters more than the parts: substance, crunch, and something sharp to cut through.

Storage

Snack plates do not keep assembled. Store components separately in the fridge: cheese wrapped, crackers sealed, anything cut held airtight for up to two days.

Slop Notes

Tear the burrata open at the table so the cream spills out. Only works with ripe, in season peaches.
