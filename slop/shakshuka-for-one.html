Slop of the Day — June 17, 2026

SHAKSHUKA
FOR ONE.

Published June 17, 2026
Eggs Spicy Warm
Shakshuka for One — foidslop

Eggs poached in spiced tomato sauce. A dramatic, satisfying pan dinner.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
20m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • diced tomatoes (400g) 1 can
  • onion, diced 1/2
  • garlic, minced 2 cloves
  • cumin 1 tsp
  • smoked paprika 1 tsp
  • chilli flakes 1/2 tsp
  • eggs 2 large
  • crusty bread, feta, fresh parsley to serve

Method

  1. 01

    Build sauce

    Saute the half diced onion in olive oil until soft, 5 minutes. Add 2 cloves of minced garlic, 1 teaspoon of cumin, 1 teaspoon of smoked paprika, and half a teaspoon of chilli flakes, cooking 1 minute. Pour in the 1 can of diced tomatoes and simmer 10 minutes until thickened. Season generously.

  2. 02

    Add eggs

    Make wells in the sauce with a spoon. Crack the 2 large eggs into the wells. Cover and cook 5 to 7 minutes until whites are set but yolks runny.

  3. 03

    Serve

    Top with feta and parsley. Eat directly from the pan with crusty bread for dipping.

Slop Notes

Serve directly from the pan. It looks dramatic and saves a dish.
