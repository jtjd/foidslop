Creamy stracciatella drizzled with olive oil and black pepper. The simplest luxury.

At a Glance

Prep
3m
Cook
3m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Serve

    Spoon 80g of stracciatella onto a plate. Drizzle generously with 2 tablespoons of excellent olive oil and season with flaky salt and cracked black pepper. Serve alongside toasted sourdough for scooping.

Slop Notes

Stracciatella is the creamy filling from burrata. Many Italian delis sell it separately.
