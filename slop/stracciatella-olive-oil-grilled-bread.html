STRACCIATELLA WITH OLIVE OIL
AND GRILLED BREAD.

Stracciatella with Olive Oil and Grilled Bread / foidslop
Home/Archive/Stracciatella with Olive Oil and Grilled Bread

Creamy stracciatella drizzled with olive oil and black pepper. The simplest luxury.

There is no cooking trick hiding here. The pleasure comes from choosing good ingredients, arranging enough for a real meal, and changing the bite as you go.

At a Glance

Prep
3m
Cook
3m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Set up

    Spoon 80g of stracciatella onto a plate. Take a moment to check the texture and seasoning before moving to the next step.

  2. 02

    Finish

    Drizzle generously with 2 tablespoons of excellent olive oil and season with flaky salt and cracked black pepper. Serve alongside toasted sourdough for scooping.

Slop Notes

Stracciatella is the creamy filling from burrata. Many Italian delis sell it separately.
