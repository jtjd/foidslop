Creamy stracciatella drizzled with olive oil and black pepper. The simplest luxury.

The arrangement is part of Stracciatella with Olive Oil and Grilled Bread. Leave stracciatella or burrata cream and flaky sea salt and cracked black pepper in their own spaces, then let the creamy, crisp, and briny parts meet in different combinations.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Set up

    Spoon 80g of stracciatella onto a plate. Take a moment to check the texture and seasoning before moving to the next step.

  2. 02

    Finish

    Drizzle generously with 2 tablespoons of excellent olive oil and season with flaky salt and cracked black pepper. Serve alongside toasted sourdough for scooping.

Easy swaps

No stracciatella or burrata cream? Any cheese, hummus, or tinned fish can anchor the plate. Keep one creamy thing, one crisp thing, and one briny thing and the board still works.

Storage

Keep stracciatella or burrata cream and flaky sea salt and cracked black pepper in separate containers and assemble the plate just before eating. Refrigerate cut ingredients for up to two days, but keep crackers and crisps dry.

Slop Notes

Stracciatella is the creamy filling from burrata. Many Italian delis sell it separately.
