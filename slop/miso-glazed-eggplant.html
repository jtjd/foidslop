Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Prep eggplant

    Halve the eggplant lengthwise. Score the flesh in a crosshatch pattern. Brush with oil.

  2. 02

    Cook

    Flesh side down, cook in a pan over medium heat for 5-7 minutes until soft. Flip over.

  3. 03

    Glaze

    Mix miso, sake, sugar, and soy sauce. Spread on the flesh side. Place under the broiler for 2-3 minutes until bubbly and golden.

Slop Notes

Scoring the eggplant helps it cook evenly and absorb the glaze.
