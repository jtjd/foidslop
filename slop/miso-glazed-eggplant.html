GLAZED EGGPLANT.

Published September 20, 2026

Miso Glazed Eggplant — foidslop
Home / Archive / Miso Glazed Eggplant

Soft, custardy eggplant broiled with a sweet and savory miso glaze.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
15m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Mid

Ingredients

  • eggplant 1 large
  • white miso paste 2 tbsp
  • sake or water 1 tbsp
  • sugar 1 tbsp
  • soy sauce 1 tsp
  • oil 1 tbsp

Method

  1. 01

    Prep eggplant

    Halve the eggplant lengthwise. Score the flesh in a crosshatch pattern. Brush with oil.

  2. 02

    Cook

    Flesh side down, cook in a pan over medium heat for 5-7 minutes until soft. Flip over.

  3. 03

    Glaze

    Mix miso, sake, sugar, and soy sauce. Spread on the flesh side. Place under the broiler for 2-3 minutes until bubbly and golden.

Slop Notes

Scoring the eggplant helps it cook evenly and absorb the glaze.
