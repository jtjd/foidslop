An avocado half filled with mozzarella, tomato, basil, and balsamic makes a fresh single-serving salad.

Caprese Stuffed Avocado works because the ingredients keep their own texture. Cut ripe avocado into fork-friendly pieces, dress lightly, and let the sharper flavors settle for a minute.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Prep avocado

    Halve the avocado and remove the pit. Scoop out a little flesh to make the hole bigger.

  2. 02

    Stuff

    Toss the tomatoes, mozzarella, and basil with olive oil. Spoon the mixture into the avocado halves. Drizzle with balsamic glaze and season.

Easy swaps

Greens swap freely by season. Without ripe avocado, toasted nuts or seeds restore the crunch the salad is counting on.

Storage

Make the components ahead, but do not finish the salad until serving. Refrigerate cherry tomatoes separately so the bowl keeps its snap.

Slop Notes

Choose an avocado that gives slightly under gentle pressure so it holds the filling without feeling hard.
