CLASSIC CHEESE
QUESADILLA.

The comfort food that requires no recipe. Just cheese, a tortilla, and heat.

Let the main ingredient in Classic Cheese Quesadilla do the heavy lifting. Keep Oaxacan cheese or Monterey Jack recognizable, add salsa after the heat, and serve before the best texture fades.

No apology needed

Use a dry pan and medium heat so the tortilla has time to color before the cheese turns oily. Keep the cheese in an even layer and leave a little room at the edge for the fold to seal. A thin smear of refried beans makes the center more substantial without making it wet. Rest the quesadilla briefly before cutting so the filling stays inside.

The quesadilla is the kind of food that needs no explanation, which may be why the internet keeps trying to give it one. It can be childhood comfort, late-night food, or just lunch. Women do not need to make familiar food more refined before it is allowed to count as a meal.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Fill

    Scatter the 80g of shredded cheese over one half of the 1 large flour tortilla and fold it over.

  2. 02

    Cook

    Heat a dry pan over medium heat. Cook the quesadilla for 2 to 3 minutes per side until golden and the cheese is fully melted. Slice into wedges and serve with 1 tablespoon of salsa and 2 tablespoons of sour cream.

Easy swaps

Protein here is a suggestion, not a rule. Swap Oaxacan cheese or Monterey Jack freely and let the sauce and timing lead; check doneness a few minutes early either way.

Storage

Cool the cooked portion promptly and keep it covered for three days. Warm it at moderate heat, then add salsa after reheating so it keeps its texture.

Slop Notes

The most underrated move is a light smear of refried beans before the cheese.
