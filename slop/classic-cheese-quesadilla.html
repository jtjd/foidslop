Slop of the Day — May 31, 2026

CLASSIC
CHEESE QUESADILLA.

Published May 31, 2026
Quick Comfort Cheese
Classic Cheese Quesadilla — foidslop
Home / Archive / Classic Cheese Quesadilla

The comfort food that requires no recipe. Just cheese, a tortilla, and heat.

At a Glance

Prep
2m
Cook
5m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • flour tortilla 1 large
  • Oaxacan cheese or Monterey Jack, shredded 80g
  • salsa 1 tbsp
  • sour cream 2 tbsp

Method

  1. 01

    Fill

    Scatter the 80g of shredded cheese over one half of the 1 large flour tortilla and fold it over.

  2. 02

    Cook

    Heat a dry pan over medium heat. Cook the quesadilla for 2 to 3 minutes per side until golden and the cheese is fully melted. Slice into wedges and serve with 1 tablespoon of salsa and 2 tablespoons of sour cream.

Slop Notes

The most underrated move is a light smear of refried beans before the cheese.
