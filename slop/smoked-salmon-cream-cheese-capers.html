Deli style but make it dinner. Everything a bagel would be without the bagel.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Arrange

    Lay the 80g of smoked salmon slices on one side of a plate. Put the 3 tablespoons of cream cheese in a small bowl on the opposite side. Scatter 1 tablespoon of capers and the very thinly sliced red onion over the salmon. Add a handful of rye crackers, a few sprigs of fresh dill, and a lemon wedge for squeezing. Build little cracker sandwiches as you eat.

Slop Notes

Let red onion soak in a splash of lemon juice for 10 minutes to mellow the bite.
