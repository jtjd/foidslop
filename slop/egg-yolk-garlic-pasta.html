EGG YOLK
GARLIC PASTA.

Garlic pasta finished with an egg yolk. A solo carbonara for when you cannot be bothered.

Egg Yolk Garlic Pasta comes together when the noodles finish in the sauce. Keep garlic close, save some cooking water, and stop as soon as the bowl turns glossy.

At a Glance

Prep
3m
Cook

Serves
1

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Cook pasta and garlic

    Boil 100g of pasta in salted water. Toast 3 cloves of sliced garlic in 2 tablespoons of olive oil until golden, saving pasta water.

  2. 02

    Combine

    Toss hot pasta with the oil and a splash of pasta water off heat. Let cool slightly, then add 1 egg yolk and toss rapidly to create a creamy sauce.

  3. 03

    Finish

    Add 30g of grated parmesan, toss, and season with chilli flakes and black pepper.

Easy swaps

Different short pasta shapes swap freely here. No garlic? Pecorino, asiago, or extra-black-pepper parmesan covers the same role in the sauce.

Storage

The sauce keeps covered for two days, while the pasta is best revived in a pan over low heat. Add egg yolk after warming so its flavor stays clear.

Slop Notes

Letting the pasta cool very slightly before adding the yolk is what prevents scrambled eggs.
