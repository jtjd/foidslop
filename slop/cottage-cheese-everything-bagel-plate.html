Creamy cottage cheese, juicy tomatoes, and everything bagel seasoning make a savory plate with plenty of crunch.

Treat Cottage Cheese and Everything Bagel Plate like a small composed meal. Put full fat cottage cheese at the center, use everything bagel seasoning to keep the plate lively, and add the sharp or salty finish just before eating.

Method

  1. 01

    Plate

    Scoop the 1 cup of full fat cottage cheese into a wide, shallow bowl. Use the back of a spoon to create a deep divot in the center of the cheese. Pour a generous drizzle of good olive oil directly into the divot. Shower the top with 1 teaspoon of everything bagel seasoning so the seeds fall into the oil and cheese. Arrange a handful of halved cherry tomatoes around the edges and serve with crackers or cucumber slices for scooping.

Easy swaps

Swap full fat cottage cheese for whatever is already open in the fridge, and let everything bagel seasoning keep its original contrast. The formula matters more than the parts: substance, crunch, and something sharp to cut through.

Storage

The assembled plate loses its contrast in the fridge. Chill full fat cottage cheese in a covered container, keep everything bagel seasoning sealed on its own, and rebuild the portions at serving time.

Slop Notes

Add chilli crisp instead of olive oil for a spicy upgrade. Trader Joe's Everything But The Bagel seasoning is the move.
