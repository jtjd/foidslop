Sweet ripe mango sliced and dusted with Tajin and lime. The refreshing solo dessert dinner.

Mango with Tajin and Lime has a clear payoff for very little work. Keep the components recognizable, taste before serving, and let Tajin seasoning provide the final lift.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Slice

    Stand the mango upright and cut the flesh away from both sides of the pit. Score or peel the pieces, then cut them into slices or bite-size cubes.

  2. 02

    Season

    Arrange on a plate. Squeeze half a lime over, dust generously with 1 tsp Tajin. Drizzle with chamoy if using.

Easy swaps

Ripe mango can become whatever you have on hand. Keep portions honest for one and season louder than you think for such a small plate.

Storage

Keep ripe mango and Tajin seasoning in separate containers if saving a portion. Refrigerate overnight at most, then restore the crunch or warmth before serving.

Slop Notes

The mango must be ripe. Under ripe mango with chilli is just confusing.
