WITH PITA.

A spicy, smoky roasted red pepper and walnut dip. Better than hummus.

The texture of Muhammara Dip with Pita should be soft enough to scoop but sturdy enough to hold a swirl. Let pita bread provide the crunch and serve it before the edges dry out.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Plate

    Spoon the muhammara into a shallow bowl. Drizzle with olive oil. Serve with torn warm pita. Scatter with walnuts and mint if desired.

Easy swaps

Out of pita bread? Use toasted pita, sliced cucumber, sturdy crackers, or crisp vegetables for scooping. Keep the dip and scoops separate until serving.

Storage

This dip keeps covered for four days. Let one portion lose its chill, stir in a teaspoon of water if needed, and add pita bread only when serving.

Slop Notes

Muhammara is made with roasted red peppers, walnuts, and pomegranate molasses. It is tangy and deeply flavorful.
