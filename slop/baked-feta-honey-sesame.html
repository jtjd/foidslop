BAKED
FETA WITH HONEY AND SESAME.

Published September 12, 2026
Fancy Sweet-Savory Cheese
Baked Feta with Honey and Sesame — foidslop
Home / Archive / Baked Feta with Honey and Sesame

A block of feta baked until soft, then drenched in honey and sesame seeds.

At a Glance

Prep
2m
Cook
15m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • block feta cheese 100g
  • olive oil 1 tbsp
  • honey 2 tbsp
  • sesame seeds 1 tsp
  • warm pita or crusty bread to serve

Method

  1. 01

    Bake

    Preheat oven to 400F/200C. Place the feta in a small baking dish. Drizzle with olive oil. Bake for 15 minutes until soft and golden on top.

  2. 02

    Dress

    Remove from oven. Immediately drizzle with honey and sprinkle with sesame seeds. Serve with warm pita.

Slop Notes

The contrast of the salty, warm cheese with the sweet honey is incredible.
