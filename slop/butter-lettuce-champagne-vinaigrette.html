Home/Archive/Butter Lettuce with Champagne Vinaigrette

The lightest, most elegant salad. Butter lettuce with a bright, acidic champagne dressing.

The method is mostly about balance. Taste for acid, salt, and richness, then adjust until the bowl feels lively enough to eat on its own.

At a Glance

Prep
8m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Make vinaigrette

    Whisk 1 tbsp vinegar, 1 tsp dijon, and 1 clove of minced garlic together. Slowly stream in 3 tbsp olive oil until emulsified. Season with salt and white pepper.

  2. 02

    Dress and serve

    Gently toss 1 head of butter lettuce leaves with just enough dressing to coat lightly. Plate immediately and add parmesan or hazelnuts.

Slop Notes

Underdress rather than overdress. Butter lettuce wilts quickly. Add dressing at the very last second before serving.
