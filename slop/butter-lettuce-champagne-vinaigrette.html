Slop of the Day — August 8, 2026

BUTTER
LETTUCE WITH CHAMPAGNE VINAIGRETTE.

Published August 8, 2026
Salad Fancy Light
Butter Lettuce with Champagne Vinaigrette — foidslop

The lightest, most elegant salad. Butter lettuce with a bright, acidic champagne dressing.

At a Glance

Prep
8m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • butter lettuce, leaves separated 1 head
  • champagne or white wine vinegar 1 tbsp
  • dijon mustard 1 tsp
  • garlic, minced 1 small clove
  • excellent olive oil 3 tbsp
  • salt and white pepper to taste
  • shaved parmesan, toasted hazelnuts, sliced radish optional

Method

  1. 01

    Make vinaigrette

    Whisk 1 tbsp vinegar, 1 tsp dijon, and 1 clove of minced garlic together. Slowly stream in 3 tbsp olive oil until emulsified. Season with salt and white pepper.

  2. 02

    Dress and serve

    Gently toss 1 head of butter lettuce leaves with just enough dressing to coat lightly. Plate immediately and add parmesan or hazelnuts.

Slop Notes

Underdress rather than overdress. Butter lettuce wilts quickly. Add dressing at the very last second before serving.
