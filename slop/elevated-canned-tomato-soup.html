ELEVATED CANNED
TOMATO SOUP.

Turn one can of tomato soup into a richer bowl with cream, butter, basil oil, and crisp crackers.

Warm canned tomato soup over medium-low heat so the cream stays smooth and the bottom does not scorch. Stir in the butter after the soup is hot, then finish each bowl with basil oil and something crisp.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Heat and enrich

    Warm the 1 can of tomato soup in a saucepan over medium heat. Stir in 2 tablespoons of heavy cream and 1 tablespoon of butter until completely melted and incorporated.

  2. 02

    Serve

    Pour into a mug or bowl, drizzle with basil oil, and add crackers or croutons with flaky salt.

Easy swaps

Half-and-half or unsweetened oat cream can replace heavy cream. Use pesto or good olive oil if basil oil is unavailable. Grilled bread, croutons, or a cheese toast can stand in for crackers.

Storage

Refrigerate leftover soup in a sealed container for up to three days. Reheat over medium-low heat, stirring often, and avoid a hard boil so the dairy does not separate.

Slop Notes

Taste before seasoning because canned soup brands differ in salt and sweetness. A small squeeze of lemon can sharpen a soup that tastes flat.
