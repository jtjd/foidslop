Elevated Canned Tomato Soup / foidslop
Home/Archive/Elevated Canned Tomato Soup

Good canned tomato bisque made significantly better with cream, butter, and basil oil.

Use a small saucepan and keep the simmer steady rather than aggressive. Taste at the end, when the broth has reduced and all the salty ingredients are in the pot.

At a Glance

Prep
2m
Cook
8m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Heat and enrich

    Warm the 1 can of tomato soup in a saucepan over medium heat. Stir in 2 tablespoons of heavy cream and 1 tablespoon of butter until completely melted and incorporated.

  2. 02

    Serve

    Pour into a mug or bowl, drizzle with basil oil, and add crackers or croutons with flaky salt.

Slop Notes

The cream and butter addition sounds minor but genuinely transforms any canned soup.
