PROSCIUTTO WRAPPED MELON
WITH MINT.

Published August 15, 2026

Prosciutto Wrapped Melon with Mint / foidslop
Home/Archive/Prosciutto Wrapped Melon with Mint

The classic appetizer, upgraded with fresh mint for a bright finish.

Think of this as dinner with the assembly left visible. Something rich, something briny, and something crisp keep the plate interesting without creating extra work.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Wrap

    Wrap each melon wedge with a slice of prosciutto. Arrange on a plate. Tuck fresh mint leaves between the wedges. Drizzle with balsamic glaze.

Slop Notes

The mint makes this feel like a spa day rather than a lazy dinner.
