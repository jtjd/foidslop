Slop of the Day — September 24, 2026

SMASHED
PEA AND RICOTTA TOAST.

Published September 24, 2026
Toast Vegetarian Spring
Smashed Pea and Ricotta Toast — foidslop
Home / Archive / Smashed Pea and Ricotta Toast

Bright green smashed peas over creamy ricotta on sourdough toast.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
5m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • sourdough, toasted 2 slices
  • frozen peas 1/2 cup
  • ricotta cheese 1/4 cup
  • olive oil 1 tbsp
  • lemon juice squeeze
  • salt, black pepper, mint leaves to taste

Method

  1. 01

    Cook peas

    Boil or microwave the peas until tender. Mash roughly with a fork. Mix with olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.

  2. 02

    Assemble

    Spread ricotta on the toast. Top with the smashed peas. Garnish with fresh mint leaves.

Slop Notes

This is a classic Australian cafe breakfast. It is fresh and vibrant.
