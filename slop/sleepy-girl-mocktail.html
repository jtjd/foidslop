SLEEPY GIRL
MOCKTAIL.

Tart cherry juice and sparkling water make a bright, fizzy mocktail with an optional magnesium addition.

Chill the ingredients for Sleepy Girl Mocktail first so the ice does not have to do all the work. Stir magnesium powder smooth, then add the bubbles or final garnish gently.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Mix

    Fill a glass with ice. Pour in 1/2 cup of tart cherry juice and stir in 1 scoop of magnesium powder until completely dissolved. Top with sparkling water or prebiotic soda. Garnish with a lime wedge.

Easy swaps

The base swaps freely: if magnesium powder is unavailable, any juice or syrup in the same flavor family keeps the balance. Build strong, pour over ice, adjust after one sip.

Storage

Build this drink fresh. Keep the juice or syrup base covered for up to two days, then add magnesium powder, ice, and any bubbles only at serving time.

Slop Notes

Magnesium is optional. If you use it, follow the serving directions on the package and stir until the powder is fully dissolved.
