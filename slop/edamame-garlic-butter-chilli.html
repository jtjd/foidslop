Steamed edamame tossed with garlic butter and chilli makes a salty, hands-on snack dinner.

Steam the edamame only until hot and bright green, then dry the pods before tossing so the garlic butter clings. Bloom the garlic and chilli gently and pull the pan before the garlic browns.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Cook edamame

    Microwave or boil the 1 cup of edamame pods until hot, then drain.

  2. 02

    Toss

    Melt 1 tablespoon of butter in a pan, add 1 clove of minced garlic and a pinch of chilli flakes for 30 seconds. Toss the edamame in the garlic butter and season with flaky sea salt.

Easy swaps

Missing frozen edamame in pods? Any sturdy cracker-worthy substitute works. The point is contrast between bites, not exact replication.

Storage

For leftovers, cool any cooked parts promptly and store them away from the crisp finish. Reheat frozen edamame in pods gently and add salted butter only at the table.

Slop Notes

Eat by popping the beans directly into your mouth from the pod. The tactile experience is part of it.
