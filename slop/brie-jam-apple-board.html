Ripe brie, sharp jam, crisp apple slices. The girl dinner that feels like a French picnic.

Brie with Jam and Apple on a Board works because the bite keeps changing: ripe brie or camembert gives it an anchor and fig jam or raspberry jam brings contrast. Keep the pieces distinct and build each forkful as you go.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Arrange

    Place the 1 wedge of ripe brie on a small board. Spoon 1 tablespoon of fig or raspberry jam right alongside it. Thinly slice half of a crisp apple and fan the slices out on the board. Add a handful of water biscuits. Drizzle honey over the brie and add walnuts if you want extra crunch.

Easy swaps

No ripe brie or camembert? Any cheese, hummus, or tinned fish can anchor the plate. Keep one creamy thing, one crisp thing, and one briny thing and the board still works.

Storage

Store the pieces of this plate separately for up to two days. Cover ripe brie or camembert, protect fig jam or raspberry jam from moisture, and bring everything together only when you are ready to eat.

Slop Notes

Let the brie sit at room temperature for 30 minutes first. Cold brie is a tragedy.
