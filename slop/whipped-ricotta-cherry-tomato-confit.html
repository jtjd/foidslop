Whipped Ricotta with Cherry Tomato Confit / foidslop
Home/Archive/Whipped Ricotta with Cherry Tomato Confit

Silky whipped ricotta topped with sweet, blistered cherry tomatoes in olive oil.

Use a shallow bowl so there is room for olive oil, herbs, or anything crunchy on top. Serve it with enough bread, chips, or vegetables to turn the dip into dinner.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Whip

    Add 1/2 cup of ricotta and 1 tbsp olive oil to a food processor or bowl. Whip until completely smooth and fluffy, about 2 minutes.

  2. 02

    Plate

    Spoon the whipped ricotta into a shallow bowl. Top with 1/4 cup of cherry tomato confit, letting the oil pool slightly. Season with flaky salt, black pepper, and fresh basil. Serve with crackers.

Slop Notes

If you do not have tomato confit, just halve some cherry tomatoes and toss them with a little olive oil and salt.
