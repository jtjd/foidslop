Slop of the Day — October 20, 2026

BURRATA
AND TOMATO PASTA.

Published October 20, 2026
Pasta Fancy Vegetarian
Burrata and Tomato Pasta — foidslop
Home / Archive / Burrata and Tomato Pasta

Hot pasta tossed with burst tomatoes, topped with a ball of creamy burrata.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
15m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • pasta 100g
  • cherry tomatoes 1 cup
  • fresh burrata 1 ball
  • olive oil 2 tbsp
  • garlic, minced 2 cloves
  • fresh basil handful
  • salt, chilli flakes to taste

Method

  1. 01

    Cook pasta

    Boil pasta in salted water until al dente.

  2. 02

    Blister tomatoes

    Heat olive oil in a pan. Add garlic and tomatoes. Cook until tomatoes burst, about 5 minutes.

  3. 03

    Combine

    Toss the pasta with the tomatoes. Place in a bowl. Tear the burrata over the top. Add basil, salt, and chilli flakes.

Slop Notes

The heat from the pasta will soften the burrata, creating a rich sauce.
