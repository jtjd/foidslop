Elevated canned tomato soup topped with cubed, crispy grilled cheese.

Keep the simmer for Tomato Soup with Grilled Cheese Croutons steady rather than aggressive. Let good tomato soup soften into the broth, then finish with thick bread after tasting the pot.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Make grilled cheese

    Butter the outside of the bread. Place cheese inside. Cook in a pan over medium heat until golden on both sides and cheese is melted. Cut into bite-sized cubes.

  2. 02

    Heat soup

    Warm the tomato soup in a saucepan. Stir in a splash of heavy cream.

  3. 03

    Serve

    Pour the soup into a bowl. Top with the grilled cheese croutons right before eating so they stay crunchy.

Easy swaps

No good tomato soup? Any soft vegetable roasts or simmers into the base the same way. Canned tomatoes can replace fresh in equal volume.

Storage

Cool this soup promptly and keep it covered for up to three days. Warm one portion slowly and finish with thick bread so the fresh flavor survives.

Slop Notes

This is the fun cousin of the classic tomato soup and grilled cheese combo.
