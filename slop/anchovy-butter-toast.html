Anchovy Butter Toast — foidslop
Home/Archive/Anchovy Butter Toast

For the umami obsessed. Anchovies mashed into butter on very good toast.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
3m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • oil packed anchovy fillets3-4
  • salted butter, room temperature2 tbsp
  • sourdough, toasted2 slices
  • lemonsqueeze
  • sliced radish, fresh parsley, soft boiled eggoptional

Method

  1. 01

    Make anchovy butter

    Mash 3 to 4 anchovy fillets and 2 tablespoons of butter with a fork until combined, adding a squeeze of lemon.

  2. 02

    Spread

    Spread generously on hot toast. Top with sliced radishes and parsley if using.

Slop Notes

Anchovies in butter lose their fishiness and become pure savory depth. Anyone who thinks they do not like anchovies usually likes this.
