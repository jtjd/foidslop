An elevated grilled cheese with melted brie and salty prosciutto.

The portion in Brie and Prosciutto Grilled Cheese is practical without feeling slight. Read the method once, watch for the doneness cue, and keep brie ready for the hot plate.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Build

    Butter the outside of the bread. Place brie, prosciutto, and fig jam between the unbuttered sides.

  2. 02

    Cook

    Cook in a pan over medium-low heat for 3-4 minutes per side until golden and the brie is fully melted.

Easy swaps

Protein here is a suggestion, not a rule. Swap butter freely and let the sauce and timing lead; check doneness a few minutes early either way.

Storage

Save leftovers in a sealed container for up to three days. Reheat slowly and finish with butter at the table rather than cooking the fresh element twice.

Slop Notes

The fig jam adds a sweet element that pairs perfectly with the salty prosciutto.
