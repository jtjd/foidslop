A bit unhinged but completely valid. Stovetop popcorn with real butter and fancy seasoning.

This is low effort with a clear payoff. A little heat, salt, or acidity turns the familiar ingredients into something worth sitting down for.

At a Glance

Prep
2m
Cook
5m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Pop

    Heat 2 tablespoons of neutral oil in a large pot over medium high. Add 3 test kernels and cover. When they pop, add the rest of the kernels, cover, and shake the pot every 30 seconds until popping slows.

  2. 02

    Season

    Transfer to a bowl, immediately toss with 2 tablespoons of melted butter, flaky salt, and your seasoning of choice so it sticks to the hot kernels.

Slop Notes

Stovetop popcorn stays crisp and lets you control the butter and salt. Savory nutritional yeast is especially good with chilli powder.
