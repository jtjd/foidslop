Home/Archive/Brie and Raspberry Quesadilla

Sweet savory quesadilla that sounds wrong but is absolutely right.

At a Glance

Prep
3m
Cook
5m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Build

    Spread 1 tablespoon of raspberry jam on one half of the 1 large flour tortilla. Lay 60g of thinly sliced brie on top and fold it over.

  2. 02

    Cook

    Melt 1 teaspoon of butter in a pan over medium heat. Cook the quesadilla for 2 to 3 minutes per side, pressing down with a spatula, until golden and the brie is fully melted. Cut and eat immediately.

Slop Notes

Add a thin layer of dijon on the jam side for a more grown up sweet savory balance.
