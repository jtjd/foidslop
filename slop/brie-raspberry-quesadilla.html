Sweet savory quesadilla that sounds wrong but is absolutely right.

A short ingredient list lets Brie and Raspberry Quesadilla depend on texture. Season brie in stages, cook until the center is ready, and finish with something fresh or sharp.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Build

    Spread 1 tablespoon of raspberry jam on one half of the 1 large flour tortilla. Lay 60g of thinly sliced brie on top and fold it over.

  2. 02

    Cook

    Melt 1 teaspoon of butter in a pan over medium heat. Cook the quesadilla for 2 to 3 minutes per side, pressing down with a spatula, until golden and the brie is fully melted. Cut and eat immediately.

Easy swaps

Camembert is the closest replacement for brie. Mild goat cheese or sharp cheddar also works, but use less jam with cheddar. Apricot, fig, or cherry preserves can replace raspberry jam.

Storage

Save leftovers in a sealed container for up to three days. Reheat slowly and finish with brie at the table rather than cooking the fresh element twice.

Slop Notes

Add a thin layer of dijon on the jam side for a more grown up sweet savory balance.
