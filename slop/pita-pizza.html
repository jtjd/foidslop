A 10 minute pizza using pita bread as the base. Crispy, melty, endlessly customizable.

A short ingredient list lets Pita Pizza depend on texture. Season pita bread in stages, cook until the center is ready, and finish with something fresh or sharp.

At a Glance

Prep
3m
Cook

Serves
1

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Assemble

    Spread 3 tablespoons of sauce on 1 large pita, add 60g of cheese and toppings.

  2. 02

    Bake

    Bake directly on the oven rack at 425F/220C for 8 to 10 minutes until crispy.

  3. 03

    Finish

    Drizzle with olive oil and chilli flakes. Eat immediately.

Easy swaps

Naan, a thick flour tortilla, or a split English muffin can replace pita; thinner bases need a shorter bake. Use pesto or crushed tomatoes instead of marinara and any good melting cheese instead of mozzarella.

Storage

Cool the cooked portion promptly and keep it covered for three days. Warm it at moderate heat, then add marinara or pizza sauce after reheating so it keeps its texture.

Slop Notes

Putting the pita directly on the oven rack makes the bottom incredibly crispy.
