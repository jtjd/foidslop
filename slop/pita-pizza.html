Home/Archive/Pita Pizza

A 10 minute pizza using pita bread as the base. Crispy, melty, endlessly customizable.

The recipe keeps the portion practical without making dinner feel slight. Give the main ingredient enough time to brown or soften, then finish with contrast.

At a Glance

Prep
3m
Cook
10m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Assemble

    Spread 3 tablespoons of sauce on 1 large pita, add 60g of cheese and toppings.

  2. 02

    Bake

    Bake directly on the oven rack at 425F/220C for 8 to 10 minutes until crispy.

  3. 03

    Finish

    Drizzle with olive oil and chilli flakes. Eat immediately.

Slop Notes

Putting the pita directly on the oven rack makes the bottom incredibly crispy.
