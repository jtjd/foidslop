Seasonal fruit with a few good cheeses. The no effort elegant dinner.

There is no cooking trick hiding here. The pleasure comes from choosing good ingredients, arranging enough for a real meal, and changing the bite as you go.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Arrange

    Place 1 wedge of aged cheddar and 1 wedge of brie on opposite ends of a board. Fill the gaps with seasonal fruit, nuts, and crackers. Drizzle honey over the soft cheese.

Slop Notes

Let all cheeses come to room temperature for 30 minutes before eating. Cold cheese is a waste of good cheese.
