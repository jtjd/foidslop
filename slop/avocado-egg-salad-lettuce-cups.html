AVOCADO AND EGG
SALAD LETTUCE CUPS.

Creamy mashed avocado and chopped egg in crisp lettuce. Fresh, filling, no bread needed.

The method for Avocado and Egg Salad Lettuce Cups is mostly tasting. Start with the dressing, add eggs, and keep enough ripe avocado in reserve to restore crunch and brightness at the end.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Make filling

    Combine 1 mashed ripe avocado, 2 chopped hard boiled eggs, 1 teaspoon of dijon, lemon juice, and 2 finely diced celery stalks. Season well.

  2. 02

    Fill cups

    Spoon the mixture into 4 to 6 crisp butter lettuce leaves and dust with paprika.

Easy swaps

Greens swap freely by season. Without eggs, toasted nuts or seeds restore the crunch the salad is counting on.

Storage

Store the dressed and crisp parts separately for up to two days. Keep eggs covered, protect ripe avocado from the dressing, and combine at the table.

Slop Notes

The avocado supplies all the creaminess the filling needs. Keep the lettuce leaves cold and dry so they stay crisp.
