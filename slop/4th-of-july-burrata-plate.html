Slop of the Day — July 4, 2026

4TH
OF JULY BURRATA PLATE.

Published July 4, 2026
No Cook Fancy Holiday
4th of July Burrata Plate — foidslop
Home / Archive / 4th of July Burrata Plate

Red, white, and blue on a plate. Creamy burrata, sweet strawberries, and blueberries for a festive, no cook girl dinner.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • fresh burrata 1 ball
  • strawberries, hulled and halved 1/2 cup
  • fresh blueberries 1/4 cup
  • crackers or toasted baguette slices handful
  • balsamic glaze drizzle
  • good olive oil drizzle
  • flaky sea salt and black pepper to taste
  • fresh mint or basil leaves optional

Method

  1. 01

    Arrange

    Place the burrata in the center of a plate. Arrange the halved strawberries and blueberries around it in distinct sections to emphasize the red, white, and blue colors. Tuck in fresh mint or basil leaves if using.

  2. 02

    Dress and Serve

    Drizzle the burrata and fruit generously with olive oil and balsamic glaze. Season with flaky sea salt and black pepper. Serve immediately with crackers or toasted baguette for scooping.

Slop Notes

The ultimate 4th of July girl dinner. No cooking, just aesthetic snacking. Use fresh, in-season berries for the best flavor and sweetness.
