Smoky trout flaked over crisp apple slices with a mustard dressing.

There is no cooking trick hiding here. The pleasure comes from choosing good ingredients, arranging enough for a real meal, and changing the bite as you go.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Make dressing

    Whisk together 1 tbsp dijon, 1 tsp honey, 1 tbsp olive oil, and a squeeze of lemon juice.

  2. 02

    Plate

    Arrange the apple slices on a plate. Flake the smoked trout over the apples. Drizzle with the honey mustard dressing.

Slop Notes

Smoked trout is usually found in tins near the smoked salmon. It is cheaper and very flavorful.
