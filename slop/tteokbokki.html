TTEOKBOKKI.

Published August 4, 2026
KoreanSpicyComfort
Tteokbokki / foidslop
Home/Archive/Tteokbokki

Korean rice cakes in spicy sweet gochujang sauce. The ultimate comfort street food.

This is straightforward food with a few details doing the heavy lifting. Season in stages, look for the doneness cue, and add the fresh finish at the end.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
15m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Make sauce

    Combine 2 tbsp gochujang, 1 tbsp soy, and 1 tbsp sugar in a pan with 1 cup of water. Stir over medium heat until combined.

  2. 02

    Cook rice cakes

    Add 200g of rice cakes and simmer 8 to 10 minutes until soft and the sauce reduces to a thick glaze.

  3. 03

    Serve

    Spoon the rice cakes and plenty of sauce into a shallow bowl. Finish with sliced spring onions and sesame seeds, then eat while the sauce is glossy and the rice cakes are soft and chewy.

Slop Notes

Soak frozen rice cakes in cold water for 20 minutes before cooking to soften them.
