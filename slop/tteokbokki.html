TTEOKBOKKI.

Chewy Korean rice cakes simmered in a glossy, spicy-sweet gochujang sauce, scaled for one.

Soak refrigerated or frozen rice cakes until they bend without cracking, then simmer rather than boil aggressively. The sauce is ready when it clings to each rice cake in a glossy coat but still moves across the pan.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Make sauce

    Combine 2 tbsp gochujang, 1 tbsp soy, and 1 tbsp sugar in a pan with 1 cup of water. Stir over medium heat until combined.

  2. 02

    Cook rice cakes

    Add 200g of rice cakes and simmer 8 to 10 minutes until soft and the sauce reduces to a thick glaze.

  3. 03

    Serve

    Spoon the rice cakes and plenty of sauce into a shallow bowl. Finish with sliced spring onions and sesame seeds, then eat while the sauce is glossy and the rice cakes are soft and chewy.

Easy swaps

Flat rice-cake slices work in place of cylinders but cook faster. Add fish cake, a halved boiled egg, or napa cabbage for a fuller bowl. There is no close substitute for gochujang; reducing the amount is better than replacing it with plain hot sauce.

Storage

Refrigerate leftovers covered for up to three days. Reheat gently until hot throughout, adding a splash of water if cylindrical rice cakes or the sauce looks dry.

Slop Notes

Soak frozen rice cakes in cold water for 20 minutes before cooking. If the sauce thickens before the centers are tender, add water two tablespoons at a time and keep simmering.
