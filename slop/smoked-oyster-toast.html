Smoked Oyster Toast — foidslop

A tin of smoked oysters on buttered rye toast with hot sauce. Deeply underrated girl dinner.

At a Glance

Prep
3m
Cook
3m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • smoked oysters in oil 1 tin
  • sourdough or rye, toasted 2 slices
  • salted butter 2 tbsp
  • hot sauce, lemon juice, cracked pepper to taste
  • cream cheese, thinly sliced shallot optional

Method

  1. 01

    Toast and butter

    Toast 2 slices of bread until deeply golden. Butter generously with 2 tbsp of butter.

  2. 02

    Top

    Lay 1 tin of smoked oysters on toast. Add hot sauce, a squeeze of lemon, and black pepper. Add cream cheese and shallot if using.

Slop Notes

Crown Prince or Rugen Fisch are reliable smoked oyster brands. Serve with wine for the full experience.
