Slop of the Day — May 23, 2026

CAPRESE
WITH GOOD OLIVE OIL.

Published May 23, 2026
No Cook Italian Vegetarian
Caprese with Good Olive Oil — foidslop

Three ingredients, zero cooking. Entirely dependent on quality. Get good tomatoes.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • ripe heirloom or vine tomatoes, sliced 2 large
  • fresh mozzarella, sliced 125g
  • fresh basil leaves handful
  • excellent olive oil 2 tbsp
  • flaky sea salt and black pepper to taste
  • balsamic reduction optional

Method

  1. 01

    Slice and alternate

    Slice the 2 large ripe tomatoes and 125g of fresh mozzarella into thick rounds. Alternate them on a plate, tucking fresh basil leaves between them.

  2. 02

    Dress

    Drizzle generously with 2 tablespoons of excellent olive oil. Season aggressively with flaky sea salt and black pepper. Add a balsamic reduction if you like.

Slop Notes

This is either transcendent or boring depending entirely on ingredient quality. Buy the best tomatoes you can find.
