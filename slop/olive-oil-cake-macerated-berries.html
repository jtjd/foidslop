OLIVE
OIL CAKE WITH MACERATED BERRIES.

Published August 7, 2026
Sweet Italian Dessert Dinner
Olive Oil Cake with Macerated Berries — foidslop
Home / Archive / Olive Oil Cake with Macerated Berries

A slice of Italian olive oil cake with macerated berries as dinner. Acceptable and right.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • olive oil cake, bakery or homemade 1 generous slice
  • mixed berries (strawberries, blueberries, raspberries) 1 cup
  • sugar 1 tsp
  • lemon juice 1 tsp
  • whipped cream, mascarpone, or vanilla yogurt optional

Method

  1. 01

    Macerate berries

    Toss 1 cup of berries with 1 tsp sugar and 1 tsp lemon juice for 10 minutes until they release their juice.

  2. 02

    Plate

    Serve 1 slice of cake alongside or topped with macerated berries and cream if using.

Slop Notes

Olive oil cake from a good Italian bakery is one of the great things. If you cannot find it, it is also very easy to make.
