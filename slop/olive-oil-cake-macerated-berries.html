A slice of Italian olive oil cake with macerated berries as dinner. Acceptable and right.

Olive Oil Cake with Macerated Berries has a clear payoff for very little work. Keep the components recognizable, taste before serving, and let sugar provide the final lift.

At a Glance

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Macerate berries

    Toss 1 cup of berries with 1 tsp sugar and 1 tsp lemon juice for 10 minutes until they release their juice.

  2. 02

    Plate

    Serve 1 slice of cake alongside or topped with macerated berries and cream if using.

Easy swaps

Mixed berries can become whatever you have on hand. Keep portions honest for one and season louder than you think for such a small plate.

Storage

This is best straight away. If mixed berries or sugar needs to wait, cover it separately for one day and reassemble so the crisp parts do not soften.

Slop Notes

Olive oil cake from a good Italian bakery is one of the great things. If you cannot find it, it is also very easy to make.
