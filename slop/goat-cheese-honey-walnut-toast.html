Home/Archive/Goat Cheese Toast with Honey and Walnuts

Tangy goat cheese on toasted bread with honey. Easy, satisfying, and kind of impressive.

At a Glance

Prep
3m
Cook
3m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Spread

    Spread 60g of soft goat cheese generously on the 2 slices of toasted sourdough.

  2. 02

    Top

    Drizzle with 1 tablespoon of good honey, scatter 2 tablespoons of toasted walnuts, and add fresh thyme if using.

Slop Notes

Room temperature goat cheese spreads much more easily than cold. The combination of honey and goat cheese is very nearly perfect.
