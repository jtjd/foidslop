LOADED SWEET POTATO
WITH BLACK BEANS.

Published June 26, 2026
VegetarianFillingBowl
Loaded Sweet Potato with Black Beans — foidslop
Home/Archive/Loaded Sweet Potato with Black Beans

Baked sweet potato with black beans, avocado, and lime crema. A whole satisfying meal.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
50m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Bake potato

    Rub 1 large sweet potato with oil and salt, pierce it, and bake at 425F/220C for 45 to 50 minutes until soft.

  2. 02

    Load

    Split it open, fluff the inside, and top with 1/2 can of warmed black beans, half a sliced avocado, lime seasoned sour cream, and cilantro.

Slop Notes

Season the inside of the potato with salt and a pat of butter before adding toppings.
