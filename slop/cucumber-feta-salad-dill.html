Cucumber and Feta Salad with Dill / foidslop
Home/Archive/Cucumber and Feta Salad with Dill

Cool cucumber, briny feta, fresh dill, and red wine vinegar make a crisp salad that improves as it rests.

Give the dressing a moment to reach every ingredient, then taste before adding more salt. The vegetables should stay crisp while the sharper flavors settle in.

At a Glance

Prep
10m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Prepare

    Slice the cucumber into thin rounds. Toss with crumbled feta, red wine vinegar, olive oil, and chopped dill.

  2. 02

    Dress and finish

    Season with salt and pepper. Let sit for 5 minutes for flavors to meld.

Slop Notes

This is even better if you let it sit in the fridge for 30 minutes. The cucumbers release their water and create a light dressing.
