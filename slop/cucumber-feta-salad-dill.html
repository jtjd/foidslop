CUCUMBER
AND FETA SALAD WITH DILL.

Published August 25, 2026

Cucumber and Feta Salad with Dill — foidslop
Home / Archive / Cucumber and Feta Salad with Dill

A cool, crunchy cucumber salad with briny feta and fresh dill.

At a Glance

Prep
10m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • English cucumber, sliced 1
  • feta cheese, crumbled 50g
  • red wine vinegar 1 tbsp
  • olive oil 2 tbsp
  • fresh dill, chopped handful
  • salt, black pepper to taste

Method

  1. 01

    Toss

    Slice the cucumber into thin rounds. Toss with crumbled feta, red wine vinegar, olive oil, and chopped dill. Season with salt and pepper. Let sit for 5 minutes for flavors to meld.

Slop Notes

This is even better if you let it sit in the fridge for 30 minutes. The cucumbers release their water and create a light dressing.
