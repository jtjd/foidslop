SALAD WITH DILL.

Cool cucumber, briny feta, fresh dill, and red wine vinegar make a crisp salad that improves as it rests.

Cucumber and Feta Salad with Dill works because the ingredients keep their own texture. Cut English cucumber into fork-friendly pieces, dress lightly, and let the sharper flavors settle for a minute.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Prepare

    Slice the cucumber into thin rounds. Toss with crumbled feta, red wine vinegar, olive oil, and chopped dill.

  2. 02

    Dress and finish

    Season with salt and pepper. Let sit for 5 minutes for flavors to meld.

Easy swaps

Greens swap freely by season. Without English cucumber, toasted nuts or seeds restore the crunch the salad is counting on.

Storage

Store the dressed and crisp parts separately for up to two days. Keep English cucumber covered, protect feta cheese from the dressing, and combine at the table.

Slop Notes

This is even better if you let it sit in the fridge for 30 minutes. The cucumbers release their water and create a light dressing.
