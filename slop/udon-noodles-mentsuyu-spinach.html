Chewy udon noodles in a savory Japanese dipping broth with tender spinach.

Taste the noodles in Udon Noodles in Mentsuyu with Spinach before trusting the package time. A pleasantly chewy center gives mentsuyu and the sauce something to cling to without turning the bowl soft.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Make broth

    In a small pot, combine 1/4 cup mentsuyu and 1/2 cup water. Bring to a gentle simmer.

  2. 02

    Cook noodles

    Boil the frozen udon according to package directions. In the last 30 seconds, drop in the handful of spinach to wilt. Drain well.

  3. 03

    Serve

    Place the hot noodles and spinach in a deep bowl. Pour the hot broth over the top. Garnish with spring onions and optional toppings.

Easy swaps

Sauce bases swap freely if you are out of mentsuyu: peanut butter thinned with hot water, tahini plus soy, or simply more chilli oil each carry a bowl.

Storage

Refrigerate the noodles for up to one day and protect mentsuyu and baby spinach from moisture. They can be eaten cold or revived briefly in hot water before serving.

Slop Notes

Mentsuyu is a concentrated Japanese soup base found in bottles at Asian markets. It is a pantry staple for fast meals.
