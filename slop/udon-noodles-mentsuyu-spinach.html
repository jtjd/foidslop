Home/Archive/Udon Noodles in Mentsuyu with Spinach

Chewy udon noodles in a savory Japanese dipping broth with tender spinach.

This is a fast bowl with enough salt, heat, and richness to taste considered. The key is coating every strand rather than leaving the sauce pooled underneath.

At a Glance

Prep
2m
Cook
8m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Make broth

    In a small pot, combine 1/4 cup mentsuyu and 1/2 cup water. Bring to a gentle simmer.

  2. 02

    Cook noodles

    Boil the frozen udon according to package directions. In the last 30 seconds, drop in the handful of spinach to wilt. Drain well.

  3. 03

    Serve

    Place the hot noodles and spinach in a deep bowl. Pour the hot broth over the top. Garnish with spring onions and optional toppings.

Slop Notes

Mentsuyu is a concentrated Japanese soup base found in bottles at Asian markets. It is a pantry staple for fast meals.
