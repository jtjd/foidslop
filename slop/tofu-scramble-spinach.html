A vegan alternative to scrambled eggs, with soft tofu and wilted spinach.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Cook

    Heat olive oil in a pan. Crumble the tofu into the pan. Add turmeric, nutritional yeast, and soy sauce. Cook for 3-4 minutes.

  2. 02

    Add spinach

    Stir in the spinach and cook until wilted, about 1 minute. Season with salt and pepper.

Slop Notes

The turmeric gives it a yellow color, and the nutritional yeast adds a cheesy flavor.
