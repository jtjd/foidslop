Cucumber Cream Cheese Bites / foidslop
Home/Archive/Cucumber Cream Cheese Bites

Refreshing, crunchy little bites. A snack that feels like an appetizer.

This is low effort with a clear payoff. A little heat, salt, or acidity turns the familiar ingredients into something worth sitting down for.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Top

    Slice the 1 large English cucumber into thick rounds. Spread a small dollop of cream cheese on each round. Dust with 1 teaspoon of everything bagel seasoning. Top with smoked salmon, capers, or sliced radish if you want to make them fancy.

Slop Notes

Make these when you want something crunchy and satisfying with zero effort.
