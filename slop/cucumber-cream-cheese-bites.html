CUCUMBER CREAM
CHEESE BITES.

Refreshing, crunchy little bites. A snack that feels like an appetizer.

Cut the cucumber into thick, dry rounds so they stay rigid under the topping. Season the cream cheese before spooning it on, then add capers, radish, or smoked salmon only at the end.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Top

    Slice the 1 large English cucumber into thick rounds. Spread a small dollop of cream cheese on each round. Dust with 1 teaspoon of everything bagel seasoning. Top with smoked salmon, capers, or sliced radish if you want to make them fancy.

Easy swaps

Missing English cucumber? Any sturdy cracker-worthy substitute works. The point is contrast between bites, not exact replication.

Storage

For leftovers, cool any cooked parts promptly and store them away from the crisp finish. Reheat English cucumber gently and add cream cheese or whipped cream cheese only at the table.

Slop Notes

Make these when you want something crunchy and satisfying with zero effort.
