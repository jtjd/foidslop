Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Marinate

    Toss the paneer cubes in the tikka masala marinade. Let sit for 10 minutes.

  2. 02

    Skewer

    Thread the paneer, bell pepper, and onion onto skewers.

  3. 03

    Cook

    Heat oil in a pan. Cook the skewers for 2-3 minutes per side until the paneer is golden and the vegetables are charred.

Slop Notes

Paneer is a firm Indian cheese that does not melt. It is perfect for grilling.
