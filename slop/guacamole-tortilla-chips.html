A single-avocado guacamole with lime, red onion, jalapeño, cilantro, and crisp tortilla chips.

A ripe avocado should yield gently at the stem end without feeling hollow or mushy. Mash it only until chunky, season before adding the onion and jalapeño, and keep the chips separate until each bite is scooped.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Mash

    Halve the 1 large ripe avocado, remove the pit, and scoop the flesh into a bowl. Mash with a fork to your preferred texture.

  2. 02

    Season

    Add the juice of 1/4 lime, 2 tablespoons of finely diced red onion, 1 tablespoon of minced jalapeño, cilantro, and a generous pinch of salt. Taste and adjust, then serve immediately with tortilla chips.

Easy swaps

Use lemon if lime is unavailable, scallion instead of red onion, or a pinch of chilli flakes instead of jalapeño. For a larger meal, add black beans, crumbled queso fresco, or a fried egg alongside the guacamole.

Storage

Guacamole is best the day it is made. Press reusable wrap directly against the surface and refrigerate for up to one day; the close contact slows browning. Keep tortilla chips sealed separately.

Slop Notes

Add half the lime first, then taste. Too much acid can flatten the avocado flavor, while enough salt should make the lime and chile taste brighter.
