Homemade guacamole in five minutes. Because you deserve better than the tub.

This is more satisfying when the dip and its scoops are treated as one recipe. Keep the center creamy, the edges well seasoned, and the bread or chips close by.

At a Glance

Prep
8m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Mash

    Halve the 1 large ripe avocado, remove the pit, and scoop the flesh into a bowl. Mash with a fork to your preferred texture.

  2. 02

    Season

    Add the juice of 1/4 lime, 2 tablespoons of finely diced red onion, 1 tablespoon of minced jalapeño, cilantro, and a generous pinch of salt. Taste and adjust, then serve immediately with tortilla chips.

Slop Notes

The lime juice both flavors the guac and prevents browning. Add it before anything else.
