SAVORY COTTAGE
CHEESE BOWL.

Cottage cheese topped with cucumber, tomatoes, everything seasoning, and chilli crisp makes a savory bowl in five minutes.

Build Savory Cottage Cheese Bowl in layers instead of stirring it into one texture. Let full fat cottage cheese bring substance and cucumber bring brightness, then add the crunchy finish at the last moment.

The bowl without the wellness act

Use a wide bowl and season the cottage cheese itself before adding anything else. That gives the cucumber and tomatoes a savory base instead of leaving all the salt on top. Keep the vegetables fairly chunky so they stay crisp. Chilli crisp works best as a finishing drizzle, where its fried bits keep their texture.

Cottage cheese has been pulled into the content machine so often that it can start to look like a product category instead of lunch. This version does not need a promise attached to it. It is cool, salty, crunchy, and fast. Add toast or an extra egg if that is what makes the bowl enough for you.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Bowl it

    Scoop 1 cup of cottage cheese into a wide bowl. Arrange half a diced cucumber and a handful of halved cherry tomatoes on top. Season with 2 tablespoons of everything bagel seasoning, drizzle with olive oil or chilli crisp.

Easy swaps

Rice, farro, couscous, or frozen grains all make the base. No full fat cottage cheese? Any roasted vegetable or canned bean drops straight in.

Storage

Refrigerate leftovers for up to three days with wet elements separate when possible. Reheat the base gently, then add full fat cottage cheese and cucumber after warming.

Slop Notes

Good Cultures or Kalona Supernatural cottage cheese are notably better than generic brands.
