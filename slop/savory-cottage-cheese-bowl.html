CHEESE BOWL.

Savory Cottage Cheese Bowl / foidslop
Home/Archive/Savory Cottage Cheese Bowl

Cottage cheese topped with cucumber, tomatoes, everything seasoning, and chilli crisp makes a savory bowl in five minutes.

A bowl like this should be easy but not flat. Keep the creamy, crisp, and savory parts distinct, then bring them together one spoonful at a time.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Bowl it

    Scoop 1 cup of cottage cheese into a wide bowl. Arrange half a diced cucumber and a handful of halved cherry tomatoes on top. Season with 2 tablespoons of everything bagel seasoning, drizzle with olive oil or chilli crisp.

Slop Notes

Good Cultures or Kalona Supernatural cottage cheese are notably better than generic brands.
