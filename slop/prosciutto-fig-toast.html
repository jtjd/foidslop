Salty cured meat and sweet figs on ricotta toast. A two minute fancy moment.

Good toast is a small exercise in timing. Get the bread crisp, keep the topping generous, and add anything wet at the last minute so the structure holds.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
3m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Toast

    Spread 3 tablespoons of ricotta on toasted sourdough until the surface is golden and the edges are crisp. Move it to a plate while it is still hot.

  2. 02

    Top

    Layer 2 slices of prosciutto and sliced figs. Drizzle with honey or balsamic glaze and season with flaky salt and pepper.

Slop Notes

Fresh figs in season are extraordinary here. Out of season, good dried figs or fig jam both work.
