Day old rice fried until crispy with soy, egg, and sesame. The solo dinner staple.

Have eggs and soy sauce ready before the rice for Crispy Fried Rice hits the pan. Quick movement keeps the ingredients distinct while high heat builds flavor.

At a Glance

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Fry rice

    Press 1 cup of cold rice into a hot pan with 2 tbsp oil and let it crisp without stirring. Push aside, scramble 2 eggs, and fold through.

  2. 02

    Add eggs

    Add 2 tsp soy sauce, toss, and finish with 1 tsp sesame oil and 2 sliced spring onions.

Easy swaps

Brown rice, quinoa, or cauliflower rice all work. Brown the base properly before adding eggs; that toastiness is the whole trick.

Storage

Store the rice and toppings covered for up to three days. Reheat in a wide pan so eggs can regain some of its edges instead of steaming soft.

Slop Notes

Cold day old rice is non negotiable. Fresh rice is too wet and will steam instead of fry.
