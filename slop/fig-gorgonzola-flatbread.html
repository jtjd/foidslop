Fig and Gorgonzola Flatbread / foidslop
Home/Archive/Fig and Gorgonzola Flatbread

Sweet figs and sharp gorgonzola melt together on a crisp flatbread finished with honey and black pepper.

This is straightforward food with a few details doing the heavy lifting. Season in stages, look for the doneness cue, and add the fresh finish at the end.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
10m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Assemble

    Place the flatbread on a baking sheet. Top with crumbled gorgonzola and sliced figs.

  2. 02

    Bake

    Bake at 400F/200C for 8-10 minutes until the cheese is melted and the crust is crispy.

  3. 03

    Finish

    Drizzle with honey and top with a handful of fresh arugula.

Slop Notes

The peppery arugula cuts the richness of the gorgonzola perfectly.
