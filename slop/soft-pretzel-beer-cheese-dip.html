Slop of the Day — May 20, 2026

SOFT
PRETZEL WITH BEER CHEESE DIP.

Published May 20, 2026
Snack Comfort Dip
Soft Pretzel with Beer Cheese Dip — foidslop

Warm soft pretzels and a quick melted cheese sauce. Salty, satisfying, no real effort.

At a Glance

Prep
3m
Cook
10m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • soft pretzels, frozen or store bought, warmed 2-3
  • sharp cheddar, shredded 60g
  • butter 1 tbsp
  • flour 1 tsp
  • beer or milk 1/4 cup
  • dijon mustard 1 tsp

Method

  1. 01

    Make cheese sauce

    Melt 1 tablespoon of butter in a small saucepan over medium heat. Whisk in 1 teaspoon of flour to make a roux and cook for 1 minute. Slowly whisk in a quarter cup of beer or milk until smooth. Add 60g of shredded sharp cheddar and 1 teaspoon of dijon mustard, stirring continuously until the cheese is completely melted and glossy.

  2. 02

    Serve

    Warm the 2 to 3 soft pretzels according to the package instructions. Dip them straight into the pot of warm cheese sauce.

Slop Notes

A splash of hot sauce in the cheese sauce makes it noticeably better.
