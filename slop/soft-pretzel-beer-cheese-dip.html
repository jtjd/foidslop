SOFT PRETZEL WITH
BEER CHEESE DIP.

Published May 20, 2026
SnackComfortDip

SharePin itPost

Warm soft pretzels and a quick melted cheese sauce. Salty, satisfying, no real effort.

Warm the pretzel until its crust is crisp and the center is soft, then make the cheese sauce over moderate heat. Add the cheddar by handfuls and stop heating as soon as the sauce is smooth so it does not turn grainy.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Make cheese sauce

    Melt 1 tablespoon of butter in a small saucepan over medium heat. Whisk in 1 teaspoon of flour to make a roux and cook for 1 minute. Slowly whisk in a quarter cup of beer or milk until smooth. Add 60g of shredded sharp cheddar and 1 teaspoon of dijon mustard, stirring continuously until the cheese is completely melted and glossy.

  2. 02

    Serve

    Warm the 2 to 3 soft pretzels according to the package instructions. Dip them straight into the pot of warm cheese sauce.

Easy swaps

Missing soft pretzels? Any sturdy cracker-worthy substitute works. The point is contrast between bites, not exact replication.

Storage

For leftovers, cool any cooked parts promptly and store them away from the crisp finish. Reheat soft pretzels gently and add sharp cheddar only at the table.

Slop Notes

A splash of hot sauce in the cheese sauce makes it noticeably better.
