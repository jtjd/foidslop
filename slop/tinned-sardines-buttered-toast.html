TINNED SARDINES ON
BUTTERED TOAST.

Briny, rich, and done in two minutes. The girl dinner for people with great taste.

Build Tinned Sardines on Buttered Toast in the order the textures need. Get the bread crisp, season quality sardines directly, and save salted butter for the final pass so every bite has some snap.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Toast and butter

    Toast the 2 slices of sourdough until they are deeply golden and crisp. While the bread is still piping hot, spread 1 tablespoon of salted butter generously over the surface so it melts deep into the crumbs.

  2. 02

    Top

    Open the tin of sardines and lay them in a single layer over the buttered toast. Squeeze the juice of half a lemon directly over the fish. Add flaky sea salt, black pepper, and your favorite hot sauce. If you are using dijon mustard and sliced cornichons, smear the mustard on the bread before adding the sardines, and place the cornichons on top for a briny crunch.

Easy swaps

No quality sardines? Soft cheeses, mashed avocado, or ricotta play the same role, while salted butter can keep the topping interesting. Whatever the spread, season it directly instead of salting only the top.

Storage

Toast cannot be stored assembled. Keep quality sardines and salted butter covered away from the bread, then toast a fresh slice and rebuild it when you are ready.

Slop Notes

Brand matters enormously. A good tin of sardines in olive oil is a completely different thing from the cheap supermarket version.
