Slop of the Day — August 1, 2026

HONEY
GARLIC BUTTER SHRIMP.

Published August 1, 2026
Seafood Quick Sweet-Savory
Honey Garlic Butter Shrimp — foidslop
Home / Archive / Honey Garlic Butter Shrimp

Shrimp cooked in 5 minutes in a sticky honey garlic butter. Eat with bread or rice.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
8m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • raw shrimp or prawns, peeled and deveined 150g
  • garlic, minced 3 cloves
  • butter 2 tbsp
  • honey 1 tbsp
  • soy sauce 1 tbsp
  • lemon juice squeeze
  • crusty bread, rice, or noodles to serve

Method

  1. 01

    Season shrimp

    Pat 150g of shrimp dry and season with salt and pepper.

  2. 02

    Cook

    Sear in 2 tbsp butter with 3 cloves of minced garlic. Add 1 tbsp honey and 1 tbsp soy sauce, tossing until glazed. Squeeze lemon over and serve with bread.

Slop Notes

Patting shrimp completely dry before cooking is what lets them sear rather than steam.
