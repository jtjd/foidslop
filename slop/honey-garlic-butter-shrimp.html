HONEY GARLIC
BUTTER SHRIMP.

Shrimp cooked in 5 minutes in a sticky honey garlic butter. Eat with bread or rice.

The portion in Honey Garlic Butter Shrimp is practical without feeling slight. Read the method once, watch for the doneness cue, and keep garlic ready for the hot plate.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
8m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Season shrimp

    Pat 150g of shrimp dry and season with salt and pepper.

  2. 02

    Cook

    Add the shrimp and cook for 2 to 3 minutes per side, until opaque, curled, and 145°F (63°C) in the center. Pour in the honey sauce and toss for 1 minute, until it glazes the shrimp.

Easy swaps

Protein here is a suggestion, not a rule. Swap raw shrimp or prawns freely and let the sauce and timing lead; check doneness a few minutes early either way.

Storage

Refrigerate leftovers covered for up to three days. Reheat gently until hot throughout, adding a splash of water if raw shrimp or prawns or the sauce looks dry.

Slop Notes

Patting shrimp completely dry before cooking is what lets them sear rather than steam.
