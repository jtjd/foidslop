Caramelized onion dip made from scratch. Vastly superior to the packet kind.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
35m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Caramelize onions

    Cook 2 thinly sliced yellow onions in 2 tablespoons of butter with salt over low heat for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until deep golden and jammy. Let cool.

  2. 02

    Make dip

    Mix 1/2 cup of sour cream, 2 tablespoons of cream cheese, 1 teaspoon of garlic powder, and Worcestershire. Fold in the cooled onions and season.

  3. 03

    Serve

    Serve with ridged chips. The dip is better after an hour in the fridge.

Slop Notes

You cannot rush caramelized onions. Low and slow for 30 minutes minimum.
