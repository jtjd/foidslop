Caramelized onion dip made from scratch. Vastly superior to the packet kind.

Treat the dip and its scoops as one recipe. Smooth Caramelized Onion Dip with Ridged Chips, season yellow onions carefully, and keep butter close so each bite has both creaminess and crunch.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Caramelize onions

    Cook 2 thinly sliced yellow onions in 2 tablespoons of butter with salt over low heat for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until deep golden and jammy. Let cool.

  2. 02

    Make dip

    Mix 1/2 cup of sour cream, 2 tablespoons of cream cheese, 1 teaspoon of garlic powder, and Worcestershire. Fold in the cooled onions and season.

  3. 03

    Serve

    Serve with ridged chips. The dip is better after an hour in the fridge.

Easy swaps

No yellow onions? White beans, roasted eggplant, or extra yogurt blend into an equally dippable base. Taste and re-salt after blending.

Storage

This dip keeps covered for four days. Let one portion lose its chill, stir in a teaspoon of water if needed, and add butter only when serving.

Slop Notes

You cannot rush caramelized onions. Low and slow for 30 minutes minimum.
