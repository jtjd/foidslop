KIMCHI BUTTER
RAMEN.

Shin Ramyun with kimchi, butter, and a soft egg. The Korean midnight snack elevated.

Have the sauce ready before the noodles for Kimchi Butter Ramen are drained. Toss while everything is hot, then finish with kimchi so the strands stay loose and seasoned.

At a Glance

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Cook noodles

    Cook the 1 packet of noodles per instructions. In the last minute, add 1/4 cup of chopped kimchi and 1 tablespoon of kimchi juice.

  2. 02

    Finish

    Stir in 1 tablespoon of butter until melted. Fry or poach the 1 large egg separately. Top the noodles with the egg, spring onions, and sesame seeds.

Easy swaps

Sauce bases swap freely if you are out of kimchi: peanut butter thinned with hot water, tahini plus soy, or simply more chilli oil each carry a bowl.

Storage

Refrigerate the noodles for up to one day and protect kimchi and butter from moisture. They can be eaten cold or revived briefly in hot water before serving.

Slop Notes

Aged kimchi (the sour stuff in the back of the fridge) works best. Its fermented tang cuts through the richness.
