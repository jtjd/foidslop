KIMCHI BUTTER
RAMEN.

Published June 21, 2026

Kimchi Butter Ramen — foidslop
Home/Archive/Kimchi Butter Ramen

Shin Ramyun with kimchi, butter, and a soft egg. The Korean midnight snack elevated.

At a Glance

Prep
3m
Cook
6m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Cook noodles

    Cook the 1 packet of noodles per instructions. In the last minute, add 1/4 cup of chopped kimchi and 1 tablespoon of kimchi juice.

  2. 02

    Finish

    Stir in 1 tablespoon of butter until melted. Fry or poach the 1 large egg separately. Top the noodles with the egg, spring onions, and sesame seeds.

Slop Notes

Aged kimchi (the sour stuff in the back of the fridge) works best. Its fermented tang cuts through the richness.
