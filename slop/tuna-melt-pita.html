Home/Archive/Tuna Melt in a Pita

A gooey tuna melt stuffed inside a pita pocket and toasted in a pan.

A short ingredient list leaves room to pay attention to texture. Cook until the center is ready and the edges have color, then assemble while everything is hot.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
8m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Mix

    In a bowl, mix the drained tuna with mayo, dijon, and shredded cheese.

  2. 02

    Stuff and toast

    Stuff the tuna mixture into the pita pocket. Butter the outside of the pita. Cook in a pan over medium heat for 3-4 minutes per side until golden and the cheese is melted.

Slop Notes

Using a pita means no filling falls out when you flip it. It is the superior tuna melt vessel.
