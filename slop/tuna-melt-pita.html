A gooey tuna melt stuffed inside a pita pocket and toasted in a pan.

Let the main ingredient in Tuna Melt in a Pita do the heavy lifting. Keep pita bread recognizable, add tuna after the heat, and serve before the best texture fades.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Mix

    In a bowl, mix the drained tuna with mayo, dijon, and shredded cheese.

  2. 02

    Stuff and toast

    Stuff the tuna mixture into the pita pocket. Butter the outside of the pita. Cook in a pan over medium heat for 3-4 minutes per side until golden and the cheese is melted.

Easy swaps

Protein here is a suggestion, not a rule. Swap pita bread freely and let the sauce and timing lead; check doneness a few minutes early either way.

Storage

Refrigerate leftovers covered for up to three days. Reheat gently until hot throughout, adding a splash of water if pita bread or the sauce looks dry.

Slop Notes

Using a pita means no filling falls out when you flip it. It is the superior tuna melt vessel.
