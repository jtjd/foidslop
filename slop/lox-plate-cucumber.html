Slop of the Day — June 9, 2026

LOX
PLATE ON CUCUMBER.

Published June 9, 2026
No Cook Healthy Salmon
Lox Plate on Cucumber — foidslop

Low carb smoked salmon moment. All the lox flavors, no bagel required.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • English cucumber, sliced into thick rounds 1 large
  • smoked salmon (lox) 80g
  • cream cheese or labneh 3 tbsp
  • capers 1 tbsp
  • red onion, paper thin slices 1/4
  • dill few sprigs

Method

  1. 01

    Build bites

    Slice the 1 large English cucumber into thick rounds. Spread 3 tablespoons of cream cheese on each round. Top with a small piece of the 80g of lox, 1 tablespoon of capers, a paper thin slice of red onion, and a sprig of dill.

Slop Notes

These also work beautifully on endive leaves for a more elegant presentation.
