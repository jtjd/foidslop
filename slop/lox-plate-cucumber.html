Lox Plate on Cucumber / foidslop
Home/Archive/Lox Plate on Cucumber

Smoked salmon, cream cheese, capers, and cucumber deliver all the familiar lox flavors without a bagel.

The arrangement is part of the recipe because it keeps every texture distinct. Put it on a proper plate, bring the sharp and creamy things together, and take your time.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Build bites

    Slice the 1 large English cucumber into thick rounds. Spread 3 tablespoons of cream cheese on each round. Top with a small piece of the 80g of lox, 1 tablespoon of capers, a paper thin slice of red onion, and a sprig of dill.

Slop Notes

These also work beautifully on endive leaves for a more elegant presentation.
