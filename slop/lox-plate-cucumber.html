LOX PLATE
ON CUCUMBER.

Smoked salmon, cream cheese, capers, and cucumber deliver all the familiar lox flavors without a bagel.

Lox Plate on Cucumber works because the bite keeps changing: English cucumber gives it an anchor and smoked salmon brings contrast. Keep the pieces distinct and build each forkful as you go.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Build bites

    Slice the 1 large English cucumber into thick rounds. Spread 3 tablespoons of cream cheese on each round. Top with a small piece of the 80g of lox, 1 tablespoon of capers, a paper thin slice of red onion, and a sprig of dill.

Easy swaps

No English cucumber? Any cheese, hummus, or tinned fish can anchor the plate. Keep one creamy thing, one crisp thing, and one briny thing and the board still works.

Storage

Store the pieces of this plate separately for up to two days. Cover English cucumber, protect smoked salmon from moisture, and bring everything together only when you are ready to eat.

Slop Notes

These also work beautifully on endive leaves for a more elegant presentation.
