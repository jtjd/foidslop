Cottage Cheese and Pineapple Bowl — foidslop
Home / Archive / Cottage Cheese and Pineapple Bowl

A retro, sweet and savory bowl of cottage cheese with pineapple chunks.

At a Glance

Prep
3m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • full fat cottage cheese 1 cup
  • pineapple chunks (fresh or canned) 1/2 cup
  • honey 1 tbsp
  • toasted coconut flakes optional

Method

  1. 01

    Assemble

    Scoop the cottage cheese into a bowl. Top with pineapple chunks and drizzle with honey. Add coconut flakes if using.

Slop Notes

This is a retro diet classic for a reason. It is refreshing and satisfying.
