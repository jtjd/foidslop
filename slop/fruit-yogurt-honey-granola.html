Home/Archive/Fruit and Yogurt Bowl with Honey and Granola

Breakfast as dinner is always valid. Greek yogurt, fruit, honey, crunch.

The ingredients are simple, so the finish matters. Use enough salt, add something bright, and keep the crunchy pieces out of the bowl until the last minute.

At a Glance

Prep
3m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Layer

    Spoon the 1 cup of thick full fat Greek yogurt into a bowl. Top with a handful of mixed berries and a quarter cup of granola for crunch. Drizzle 1 tablespoon of honey generously over the top. Add banana slices or a spoonful of nut butter if you want to make it more filling.

Slop Notes

Full fat Greek yogurt is non negotiable. It is thick enough to hold toppings and rich enough to feel like a proper meal.
