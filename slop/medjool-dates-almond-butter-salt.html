Nature's candy stuffed with almond butter. The sweet salty snack that feels indulgent.

The method for Medjool Dates with Almond Butter and Flaky Salt is short because the ingredients already know what they are doing. Give Medjool dates enough heat or seasoning, then finish with almond butter.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Stuff

    Slice the 4 to 5 Medjool dates lengthways and remove the pits. Fill each with a spoonful of 2 tablespoons of almond butter. Pinch closed slightly.

  2. 02

    Finish

    Arrange on a plate, sprinkle with flaky sea salt, and drizzle with dark chocolate if using.

Easy swaps

Medjool dates can become whatever you have on hand. Keep portions honest for one and season louder than you think for such a small plate.

Storage

For leftovers, cool any cooked parts promptly and store them away from the crisp finish. Reheat Medjool dates gently and add almond butter only at the table.

Slop Notes

The viral date bark trend started here. These also freeze well for a cold caramel like snack.
