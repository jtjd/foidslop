Nature's candy stuffed with almond butter. The sweet salty snack that feels indulgent.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Stuff

    Slice the 4 to 5 Medjool dates lengthways and remove the pits. Fill each with a spoonful of 2 tablespoons of almond butter. Pinch closed slightly.

  2. 02

    Finish

    Arrange on a plate, sprinkle with flaky sea salt, and drizzle with dark chocolate if using.

Slop Notes

The viral date bark trend started here. These also freeze well for a cold caramel like snack.
