Whipped cream cheese and dark fruit preserves turn crisp toast into a sweet, salty five-minute dinner.

Cream Cheese and Jelly Toast depends on a properly crisp base. Toast the bread until it can support whipped cream cheese, add high quality fruit preserves at the last minute, and eat it before the topping softens the edges.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Toast

    Toast the bread until golden. Spread a thick layer of cream cheese on each slice.

  2. 02

    Top

    Top with a generous spoonful of preserves. Sprinkle with flaky salt.

Easy swaps

No whipped cream cheese? Soft cheeses, mashed avocado, or ricotta play the same role, while high quality fruit preserves can keep the topping interesting. Whatever the spread, season it directly instead of salting only the top.

Storage

Toast cannot be stored assembled. Keep whipped cream cheese and high quality fruit preserves covered away from the bread, then toast a fresh slice and rebuild it when you are ready.

Slop Notes

The flaky salt cuts the sweetness and makes the fruit flavor pop.
