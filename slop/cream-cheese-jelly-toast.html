Slop of the Day — August 16, 2026

CREAM
CHEESE AND JELLY TOAST.

Published August 16, 2026
Toast Sweet Quick
Cream Cheese and Jelly Toast — foidslop
Home / Archive / Cream Cheese and Jelly Toast

A childhood favorite, elevated with good bread and fancy jelly.

At a Glance

Prep
3m
Cook
2m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • thick brioche or sourdough, toasted 2 slices
  • whipped cream cheese 3 tbsp
  • high quality fruit preserves (fig, raspberry, or blackberry) 2 tbsp
  • flaky sea salt pinch

Method

  1. 01

    Spread

    Toast the bread until golden. Spread a thick layer of cream cheese on each slice. Top with a generous spoonful of preserves. Sprinkle with flaky salt.

Slop Notes

The flaky salt cuts the sweetness and makes the fruit flavor pop.
