Creamy, salty, sweet, and crunchy. Four textures in one bowl with zero cooking required.

The texture should be soft enough to scoop but sturdy enough to hold a swirl. Taste before garnishing because cheese, canned ingredients, and crackers can bring plenty of salt.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Whip

    Add the 150g of crumbled feta and 2 tablespoons of cream cheese to a food processor. Blend for a full 2 minutes, scraping down the sides halfway, until the mixture is completely smooth and fluffy. If it feels too stiff, add a tiny splash of water to loosen it.

  2. 02

    Plate

    Spoon the whipped feta into a shallow bowl and use the back of a spoon to create a deep swirl in the center. Drizzle 1 tablespoon of olive oil and 1 tablespoon of hot honey directly into the swirl. Scatter a handful of roughly chopped toasted walnuts over the top so they fall into the crevices. Serve immediately with crackers or crusty bread.

Easy swaps

No feta cheese? White beans, roasted eggplant, or extra yogurt blend into an equally dippable base. Taste and re-salt after blending.

Storage

Refrigerate up to four days in a sealed container. Flavor often improves overnight; stir before serving and refresh with olive oil or lemon.

Slop Notes

No food processor? Mash with a fork and beat vigorously. Less smooth but still excellent.
