EVERYTHING BAGEL
SALMON BITES.

Published July 14, 2026
No CookSalmonQuick
Everything Bagel Salmon Bites / foidslop
Home/Archive/Everything Bagel Salmon Bites

All the vibes of a bagel and lox without the bagel. Refreshing, salty, quick.

A small snack can still deserve a real plate and a little attention. Keep the crisp things crisp, the warm things warm, and do not be shy with seasoning.

At a Glance

Prep
8m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Top

    Spread 2 tablespoons of cream cheese on thick cucumber rounds. Top with 60g of smoked salmon and dust with 1 tablespoon of everything bagel seasoning. Add capers or dill.

Slop Notes

These are also great on endive spears for a slightly more elegant bite.
