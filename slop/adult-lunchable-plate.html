Exactly what you think. Crackers, deli meat, cheese, grapes, a little candy if you want.

Treat Adult Lunchable Plate like a small composed meal. Put Ritz or Triscuit crackers at the center, use deli turkey or ham to keep the plate lively, and add the sharp or salty finish just before eating.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Separate and plate

    Keep everything in distinct piles on a plate. Put the handful of crackers on one side, 3 to 4 slices of deli turkey on another, and 40g of cubed cheddar in the middle. Add grapes, baby carrots, or apple slices in the gaps. No mixing required.

Easy swaps

Swap Ritz or Triscuit crackers for whatever is already open in the fridge, and let deli turkey or ham keep its original contrast. The formula matters more than the parts: substance, crunch, and something sharp to cut through.

Storage

The assembled plate loses its contrast in the fridge. Chill Ritz or Triscuit crackers in a covered container, keep deli turkey or ham sealed on its own, and rebuild the portions at serving time.

Slop Notes

The joy is in the separateness. You eat each thing on its own or build your own stack.
