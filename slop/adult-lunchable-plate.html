Home/Archive/Adult Lunchable Plate

Exactly what you think. Crackers, deli meat, cheese, grapes, a little candy if you want.

This plate is built around contrast: creamy, crisp, salty, and fresh in every few bites. Give each ingredient its own space and assemble combinations as you eat.

At a Glance

Cook

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Separate and plate

    Keep everything in distinct piles on a plate. Put the handful of crackers on one side, 3 to 4 slices of deli turkey on another, and 40g of cubed cheddar in the middle. Add grapes, baby carrots, or apple slices in the gaps. No mixing required.

Slop Notes

The joy is in the separateness. You eat each thing on its own or build your own stack.
