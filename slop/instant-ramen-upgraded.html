INSTANT RAMEN
UPGRADED.

Upgrade instant ramen with a jammy egg, butter, sesame oil, scallions, and chilli crisp in under 10 minutes.

Use slightly less water than the packet requests so the broth stays concentrated. Butter rounds out the salty seasoning, sesame oil adds aroma, and a softly cooked egg makes the bowl substantial enough for dinner.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Cook

    Cook the 1 packet of instant ramen according to the package instructions, but use slightly less water than recommended to keep the broth concentrated and flavorful.

  2. 02

    Upgrade

    In the last 2 minutes of cooking, crack the 1 large egg directly into the bubbling broth. Remove from heat and stir in 1 tablespoon of butter, 1 teaspoon of sesame oil, and 1 teaspoon of soy sauce. Top with spring onions, sesame seeds, and chilli crisp.

Make it yours

10 ways to upgrade instant ramen

  • Peanut chilliStir in 1 tablespoon of peanut butter with the seasoning, then finish with chilli crisp and lime.
  • Kimchi butterCook 1/3 cup chopped kimchi with the noodles and replace half the finishing butter with kimchi brine.
  • Miso sesameTake the pot off the heat and dissolve 1 teaspoon of miso into the broth before adding sesame oil.
  • Corn and cheeseAdd 1/3 cup frozen corn for the final two minutes and melt a slice of American cheese into the hot broth.
  • Spinach egg-dropStir in a handful of spinach, then stream a beaten egg into the simmering broth while stirring gently.
  • Garlic mushroomBrown sliced mushrooms and one grated garlic clove in the pot before adding water and noodles.
  • Coconut curryReplace 1/3 cup of the water with coconut milk and add 1 teaspoon of curry paste before simmering.
  • Tuna mayoDrain the noodles well, then toss them with tuna, mayonnaise, a little seasoning powder, and scallions.
  • Tofu and greensAdd cubed tofu and chopped bok choy during the final three minutes, then finish with sesame seeds.
  • Crispy dry ramenDrain almost all the water and fry the cooked noodles in a little oil until the edges turn crisp.

Easy swaps

No egg? Add cubed tofu during the final two minutes or stir in a spoonful of peanut butter after cooking. Frozen corn, spinach, or peas can go into the pot with the noodles. Use half the seasoning packet if your toppings are already salty.

Storage

Instant ramen is best eaten immediately because the noodles continue absorbing broth. If necessary, refrigerate the broth and noodles separately for one day, then reheat the broth and add the noodles only long enough to warm through.

Slop Notes

Add sesame oil after the pot comes off the heat so its aroma stays intact. Taste before adding soy sauce because ramen seasoning packets vary widely in saltiness.
