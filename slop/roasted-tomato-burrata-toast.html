ROASTED TOMATO AND
BURRATA TOAST.

Slow roasted sweet tomatoes with creamy burrata on grilled bread. The best toast.

Build Roasted Tomato and Burrata Toast in the order the textures need. Get the bread crisp, season cherry tomatoes directly, and save garlic for the final pass so every bite has some snap.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Roast tomatoes

    Toss 200g of tomatoes with 3 tablespoons of oil, 1 clove of minced garlic, salt, and pepper. Roast at 400F/200C for 25 minutes until jammy.

  2. 02

    Assemble

    Top 2 slices of grilled bread with 1 torn ball of burrata. Spoon warm roasted tomatoes and all their juices over the top. Add basil, balsamic, and salt.

Easy swaps

No cherry tomatoes? Soft cheeses, mashed avocado, or ricotta play the same role, while garlic can keep the topping interesting. Whatever the spread, season it directly instead of salting only the top.

Storage

Make the components ahead, not the finished toast. Cover cherry tomatoes, protect garlic from moisture, and assemble after the bread is hot and crisp.

Slop Notes

The tomato juices and olive oil are the sauce. Make sure you get all of it out of the roasting pan.
