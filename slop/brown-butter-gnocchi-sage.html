BROWN BUTTER GNOCCHI
WITH SAGE.

Published May 30, 2026

Pillowy store bought gnocchi in nutty brown butter with crispy sage. Ready in 10 minutes.

The ingredient list for Brown Butter Gnocchi with Sage is short enough to pay attention to technique. Salt the water, brown or bloom store bought potato gnocchi, and serve before the sauce loses its lift.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Cook gnocchi

    Boil the 250g of gnocchi in salted water until they float, about 2 to 3 minutes, then drain.

  2. 02

    Brown butter

    In the same pan, melt the 3 tablespoons of salted butter over medium heat. Add the 6 to 8 fresh sage leaves. Cook until the butter turns golden brown and smells nutty, about 3 to 4 minutes.

  3. 03

    Toss

    Toss the gnocchi in the brown butter to coat. Top with 30g of grated parmesan and black pepper.

Easy swaps

Different short pasta shapes swap freely here. No store bought potato gnocchi? Pecorino, asiago, or extra-black-pepper parmesan covers the same role in the sauce.

Storage

Refrigerate leftovers in a sealed container for up to two days. Reheat gently with a splash of water so store bought potato gnocchi loosens instead of turning thick or dry.

Slop Notes

Watch the butter carefully once it starts foaming. It goes from golden to burnt very fast.
