Thin ribbons of cucumber wrapped around smoked salmon and cream cheese.

Peel the cucumber into long ribbons and blot them dry before spreading the cream cheese mixture. Keep the salmon layer thin enough that each strip can roll tightly, then add dill only at the end so it stays fresh.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Prep cucumber

    Use a vegetable peeler to slice the cucumber into long, thin ribbons.

  2. 02

    Roll

    Mix cream cheese with dijon. Spread a thin layer on a cucumber ribbon. Add a piece of smoked salmon and a sprig of dill. Roll up tightly.

Easy swaps

Smoked trout can replace salmon, and labneh or plain whipped cream cheese both work for the filling. If cucumber ribbons keep tearing, use thick cucumber rounds and build the same ingredients as open-faced bites instead.

Storage

These rolls are best assembled shortly before eating because cucumber releases water. Keep the smoked salmon, cream cheese mixture, and cucumber refrigerated separately for up to one day, then roll just before serving.

Slop Notes

If the cucumber breaks, just use shorter ribbons. They still taste amazing.
