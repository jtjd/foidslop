Slop of the Day — October 11, 2026

TOMATO
AND MOZZARELLA PANINI.

Published October 11, 2026
Quick Comfort Sandwich
Tomato and Mozzarella Panini — foidslop
Home / Archive / Tomato and Mozzarella Panini

A warm, pressed sandwich with melted mozzarella and ripe tomatoes.

At a Glance

Prep
3m
Cook
5m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • crusty bread 2 slices
  • fresh mozzarella, sliced 40g
  • cherry tomatoes, sliced handful
  • fresh basil handful
  • butter 1 tbsp
  • balsamic glaze to serve

Method

  1. 01

    Build

    Butter the outside of the bread. Place mozzarella, tomatoes, and basil between the unbuttered sides.

  2. 02

    Press

    Cook in a pan over medium heat, pressing down with a spatula or heavy lid, for 3-4 minutes per side until golden and the cheese is melted. Drizzle with balsamic glaze.

Slop Notes

If you do not have a panini press, a heavy pan on top of the sandwich works perfectly.
