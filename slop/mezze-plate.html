Hummus, tabbouleh, olives, pita. The Middle Eastern answer to the girl dinner snack plate.

There is no hidden cooking trick in Mezze Plate; the useful detail is balance. Make hummus the substantial part, keep store bought tabbouleh fresh, and serve it on a plate that gives everything room.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Arrange

    Group the 1/3 cup of hummus, 1/3 cup of tabbouleh, mixed olives, and 50g of cubed feta in separate sections of a large plate or small board. Swirl a spoon through the hummus and drizzle it with olive oil. Serve the warmed, torn pita alongside for building bites.

Easy swaps

Swap hummus for whatever is already open in the fridge, and let store bought tabbouleh keep its original contrast. The formula matters more than the parts: substance, crunch, and something sharp to cut through.

Storage

Keep hummus and store bought tabbouleh in separate containers and assemble the plate just before eating. Refrigerate cut ingredients for up to two days, but keep crackers and crisps dry.

Slop Notes

Store bought shortcuts are completely valid. Good supermarket hummus and tabbouleh are excellent.
