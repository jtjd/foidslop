Home/Archive/Soba Noodles with Sesame Soy Dressing

Cold buckwheat noodles in a nutty, savory dressing. Light but satisfying.

Noodles move quickly once they hit the water. Mix the seasonings first, keep a little cooking water nearby, and eat the finished bowl while it is at its springiest.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
8m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Cook noodles

    Boil soba noodles according to package. Drain and rinse under cold water until completely cool.

  2. 02

    Dress

    Whisk soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and sugar. Toss the cold noodles in the dressing. Top with sesame seeds and spring onions.

Slop Notes

Rinsing soba noodles thoroughly removes excess starch and gives them a perfect chewy texture.
