Cold buckwheat noodles in a nutty, savory dressing. Light but satisfying.

The trick in Soba Noodles with Sesame Soy Dressing is coating every strand instead of leaving the sauce underneath. Keep a little cooking water nearby and add soy sauce only after the noodles are tender.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Cook noodles

    Boil soba noodles according to package. Drain and rinse under cold water until completely cool.

  2. 02

    Dress

    Whisk soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and sugar. Toss the cold noodles in the dressing. Top with sesame seeds and spring onions.

Easy swaps

Instant ramen, soba, rice noodles, or wheat noodles all work interchangeably. No soy sauce? Chilli oil alone with scallions still makes the bowl interesting.

Storage

Keep leftovers covered for one day, with wet sauce separate when possible. Loosen the noodles with hot water and add soy sauce after warming.

Slop Notes

Rinsing soba noodles thoroughly removes excess starch and gives them a perfect chewy texture.
