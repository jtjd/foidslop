Crisp sourdough with whipped ricotta, honey, pistachios, flaky salt, and lemon zest, portioned for one.

Toast the bread darker than you would for breakfast so it stays crisp beneath the ricotta. Season the cheese with a pinch of salt before spreading it, then add honey sparingly enough that the pistachios still taste nutty.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Toast

    Toast the 2 slices of thick sourdough until deeply golden.

  2. 02

    Top

    Spread a generous layer of 4 tablespoons of whole milk ricotta on each slice. Drizzle with 1 tablespoon of good honey, scatter 2 tablespoons of roughly chopped pistachios, and finish with a pinch of flaky salt. Add lemon zest or fresh thyme for extra flavor.

Easy swaps

Cottage cheese blended until smooth or softened goat cheese can replace ricotta. Use almonds or walnuts instead of pistachios, and maple syrup instead of honey. Choose thick bread that can support the topping without bending.

Storage

Keep seasoned ricotta covered in the refrigerator for up to two days. Store the bread and nuts separately, then toast fresh and assemble just before eating so the base remains crisp.

Slop Notes

Whole-milk ricotta gives the creamiest result. Beat it vigorously with a fork for 30 seconds, or blend it briefly, before seasoning and spreading.
