Slop of the Day — May 25, 2026

RICOTTA
TOAST WITH HONEY AND PISTACHIOS.

Published May 25, 2026
Toast Sweet-Savory Quick
Ricotta Toast with Honey and Pistachios — foidslop

Creamy, crunchy, sweet. The toast that makes breakfast for dinner feel indulgent.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
3m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • thick sourdough or country bread, toasted 2 slices
  • whole milk ricotta 4 tbsp
  • good honey 1 tbsp
  • shelled pistachios, roughly chopped 2 tbsp
  • flaky salt pinch
  • lemon zest, fresh thyme optional

Method

  1. 01

    Toast

    Toast the 2 slices of thick sourdough until deeply golden.

  2. 02

    Top

    Spread a generous layer of 4 tablespoons of whole milk ricotta on each slice. Drizzle with 1 tablespoon of good honey, scatter 2 tablespoons of roughly chopped pistachios, and finish with a pinch of flaky salt. Add lemon zest or fresh thyme for extra flavor.

Slop Notes

The ricotta needs to be full fat and ideally fresh. Whip it with a fork first for a fluffier texture.
