Slop of the Day — May 17, 2026

GREEK
SALAD WITH CHICKPEAS.

Published May 17, 2026
Salad Vegetarian No Cook
Greek Salad with Chickpeas — foidslop

The most refreshing no cook dinner. Salty, crunchy, and genuinely satisfying.

At a Glance

Prep
8m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • chickpeas, drained and rinsed 1/2 can
  • cucumber, chunked 1/2
  • cherry tomatoes, halved handful
  • red onion, thinly sliced 1/4
  • kalamata olives handful
  • feta cheese, crumbled 50g
  • olive oil 2 tbsp
  • red wine vinegar 1 tbsp
  • dried oregano 1/2 tsp

Method

  1. 01

    Toss

    Combine the half can of drained chickpeas, chunked cucumber, halved cherry tomatoes, and thinly sliced red onion in a bowl. Drizzle with 2 tablespoons of olive oil, 1 tablespoon of red wine vinegar, and half a teaspoon of dried oregano. Season with salt and pepper and let it sit for 10 minutes so the tomatoes release their juices. Top with 50g of crumbled feta just before eating.

Slop Notes

Let it sit for 10 minutes after dressing. The tomatoes release juice that becomes the best extra dressing.
