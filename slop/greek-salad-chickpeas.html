GREEK SALAD
WITH CHICKPEAS.

Published May 17, 2026
SaladVegetarianNo Cook
Greek Salad with Chickpeas / foidslop
Home/Archive/Greek Salad with Chickpeas

The most refreshing no cook dinner. Salty, crunchy, and genuinely satisfying.

Give the dressing a moment to reach every ingredient, then taste before adding more salt. The vegetables should stay crisp while the sharper flavors settle in.

At a Glance

Prep
8m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Prepare

    Combine the half can of drained chickpeas, chunked cucumber, halved cherry tomatoes, and thinly sliced red onion in a bowl. Drizzle with 2 tablespoons of olive oil, 1 tablespoon of red wine vinegar, and half a teaspoon of dried oregano.

  2. 02

    Dress and finish

    Season with salt and pepper and let it sit for 10 minutes so the tomatoes release their juices. Top with 50g of crumbled feta just before eating.

Slop Notes

Let it sit for 10 minutes after dressing. The tomatoes release juice that becomes the best extra dressing.
