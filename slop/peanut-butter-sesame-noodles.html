SESAME NOODLES.

Sesame peanut sauce over noodles. The most satisfying thing you can make in 15 minutes.

Noodles move quickly once they hit the water. Mix the seasonings first, keep a little cooking water nearby, and eat the finished bowl while it is at its springiest.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Make sauce

    Whisk the 2 tablespoons of peanut butter, 1 tablespoon of soy sauce, 1 tablespoon of rice vinegar, 1 teaspoon of sesame oil, 1 teaspoon of honey, and sriracha. Thin with warm water until it is pourable.

  2. 02

    Cook and toss

    Boil the 100g of noodles per package, drain, and toss immediately with the sauce. Top with spring onions, sesame seeds, and cucumber ribbons.

Easy swaps

Sauce bases swap freely if you are out of peanut butter: peanut butter thinned with hot water, tahini plus soy, or simply more chilli oil each carry a bowl.

Storage

Keeps one day refrigerated, sauce separate if possible. Cold noodles are legitimate here; loosen with hot water or serve straight from the fridge.

Slop Notes

Natural peanut butter works best. The sauce keeps refrigerated for a week. Make double.
