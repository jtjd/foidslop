Crisp toast smeared with soft, sweet garlic confit and flaky salt.

A thick slice of bread turns a few strong ingredients into dinner. The useful details are browning, enough seasoning, and eating it while the toast still crackles.

At a Glance

Prep
2m
Cook
3m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Toast

    Toast the sourdough until deeply golden and crisp at the edges. Move it to a plate while it is hot so the garlic oil can soak into the rough surface.

  2. 02

    Smear

    While the toast is hot, drizzle with the garlic confit oil. Use a fork to smash the soft garlic confit cloves directly onto the bread. Sprinkle with flaky salt and fresh thyme.

Slop Notes

Garlic confit is made by slowly poaching peeled garlic cloves in olive oil over very low heat until golden and soft. Make a big batch and keep it in the fridge.
