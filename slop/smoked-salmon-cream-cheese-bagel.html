Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Toast

    Split the bagel and toast the cut sides until golden at the edges but still chewy in the middle. Let it cool for a minute so the cream cheese does not melt away.

  2. 02

    Build

    Spread cream cheese on both halves. Top with smoked salmon, capers, red onion, dill, and tomato.

Slop Notes

Using an everything bagel means you do not need any extra seasoning.
