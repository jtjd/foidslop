A sweet and savory quesadilla with sharp cheddar and crisp apple slices.

This version of Cheddar and Apple Quesadilla is scaled around one pan and one appetite. Give sharp cheddar enough time to brown or soften, then use crisp apple for contrast at the finish.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Build

    Sprinkle half the cheddar on one half of the tortilla. Top with apple slices and the rest of the cheddar. Fold over.

  2. 02

    Cook

    Melt butter in a pan. Cook the quesadilla for 2-3 minutes per side until golden and the cheese is melted. Slice and serve with honey or dijon.

Easy swaps

No sharp cheddar? Choose a filling with a similar texture, keep the same seasoning, and adjust the cooking time to its own doneness cues rather than the clock.

Storage

Refrigerate leftovers covered for up to three days. Reheat gently until hot throughout, adding a splash of water if sharp cheddar or the sauce looks dry.

Slop Notes

This sounds weird but it is essentially a grilled cheese with apple, which is a classic combo.
