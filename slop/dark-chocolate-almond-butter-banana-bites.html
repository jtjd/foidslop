Slices of banana topped with almond butter and dark chocolate chips.

Dark Chocolate and Almond Butter Banana Bites has a clear payoff for very little work. Keep the components recognizable, taste before serving, and let almond butter provide the final lift.

Method

  1. 01

    Assemble

    Slice the banana into thick rounds. Spread a small dollop of almond butter on each slice. Press a dark chocolate chip into the almond butter. Sprinkle with flaky salt.

Easy swaps

Banana can become whatever you have on hand. Keep portions honest for one and season louder than you think for such a small plate.

Storage

For leftovers, cool any cooked parts promptly and store them away from the crisp finish. Reheat banana gently and add almond butter only at the table.

Slop Notes

The flaky salt is crucial. It makes the chocolate taste more chocolatey.
