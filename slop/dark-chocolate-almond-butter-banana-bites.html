CHOCOLATE AND ALMOND BUTTER BANANA BITES.

Published August 27, 2026

Dark Chocolate and Almond Butter Banana Bites — foidslop
Home / Archive / Dark Chocolate and Almond Butter Banana Bites

Slices of banana topped with almond butter and dark chocolate chips.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • banana, sliced into rounds 1
  • almond butter 2 tbsp
  • dark chocolate chips 1 tbsp
  • flaky sea salt pinch

Method

  1. 01

    Assemble

    Slice the banana into thick rounds. Spread a small dollop of almond butter on each slice. Press a dark chocolate chip into the almond butter. Sprinkle with flaky salt.

Slop Notes

The flaky salt is crucial. It makes the chocolate taste more chocolatey.
