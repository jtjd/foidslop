Home/Archive/Creamy Mushroom Toast with Thyme

Sauteed mushrooms in garlic and thyme, splashed with cream, on thick buttered toast.

A thick slice of bread turns a few strong ingredients into dinner. The useful details are browning, enough seasoning, and eating it while the toast still crackles.

At a Glance

Prep
5m
Cook
12m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Brown the mushrooms

    Heat the butter and olive oil in a wide skillet over medium-high heat. Add the mushrooms in an even layer and leave them undisturbed for 3 to 4 minutes, until deeply browned underneath. Toss and cook for 2 minutes more.

  2. 02

    Add the aromatics

    Lower the heat to medium. Add the garlic and thyme and cook for 30 seconds, just until fragrant. Pour in the cream and simmer for 1 to 2 minutes, until it lightly coats the mushrooms.

  3. 03

    Season and serve

    Season with salt, black pepper, and a small squeeze of lemon. Spoon the creamy mushrooms over the hot buttered sourdough and serve before the toast loses its crunch.

Slop Notes

Do not stir mushrooms immediately. Letting them brown first is what creates the deep savory flavor.
