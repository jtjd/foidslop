Slop of the Day — July 19, 2026

PAN
CON TOMATE.

Published July 19, 2026
Toast No Cook Spanish
Pan Con Tomate — foidslop

The Catalan classic. Ripe tomato rubbed directly onto garlicky toasted bread.

At a Glance

Prep
3m
Cook
3m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • thick sourdough or ciabatta, toasted 2 slices
  • garlic, halved 1 clove
  • very ripe tomato, halved 1 large
  • excellent olive oil 2 tbsp
  • flaky sea salt to taste

Method

  1. 01

    Rub

    Rub 1 halved garlic clove firmly over 2 slices of hot toast. Rub 1 halved tomato over the toast, squeezing gently to stain the bread red.

  2. 02

    Finish

    Drizzle with 2 tablespoons of olive oil and flaky salt.

Slop Notes

The tomato must be very ripe and the bread must be hot. This is one of the great simple pleasures of eating.
