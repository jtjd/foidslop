The Catalan classic. Ripe tomato rubbed directly onto garlicky toasted bread.

Pan Con Tomate depends on a properly crisp base. Toast the bread until it can support garlic, add very ripe tomato at the last minute, and eat it before the topping softens the edges.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Rub

    Rub 1 halved garlic clove firmly over 2 slices of hot toast. Rub 1 halved tomato over the toast, squeezing gently to stain the bread red.

  2. 02

    Finish

    Drizzle with 2 tablespoons of olive oil and flaky salt.

Easy swaps

No garlic? Soft cheeses, mashed avocado, or ricotta play the same role, while very ripe tomato can keep the topping interesting. Whatever the spread, season it directly instead of salting only the top.

Storage

Make the components ahead, not the finished toast. Cover garlic, protect very ripe tomato from moisture, and assemble after the bread is hot and crisp.

Slop Notes

The tomato must be very ripe and the bread must be hot. This is one of the great simple pleasures of eating.
