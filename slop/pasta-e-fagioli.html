Italian peasant soup. Pasta and white beans in a rich tomato rosemary broth.

Pasta e Fagioli is a small pot, so every minute changes the texture. Build the broth in stages, stop before cannellini beans loses its shape, and serve it hot.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Build base

    Saute 2 cloves of minced garlic in 2 tablespoons of olive oil until golden. Add 1/2 can of crushed tomatoes, 1 cup of broth, 1 sprig of rosemary, and a parmesan rind. Simmer 10 minutes.

  2. 02

    Add beans and pasta

    Add half of the 1 can of beans whole and mash the rest to thicken. Add 50g of small pasta and cook until tender.

  3. 03

    Serve

    Ladle into a bowl, drizzle with oil, and add grated parmesan.

Easy swaps

Great Northern beans, navy beans, or chickpeas can replace cannellini beans. Use vegetable broth for a vegetarian bowl, and choose any small pasta shape that fits easily on a spoon.

Storage

Refrigerate in a sealed container for up to three days. Reheat gently until hot throughout, then add cannellini beans after the pot comes off the heat.

Slop Notes

Adding a parmesan rind to the pot while it simmers adds incredible depth to the broth.
