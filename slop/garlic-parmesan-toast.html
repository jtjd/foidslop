GARLIC PARMESAN
TOAST.

The simplest, most satisfying thing. Crispy garlic toast with melted parmesan.

Build Garlic Parmesan Toast in the order the textures need. Get the bread crisp, season salted butter directly, and save garlic for the final pass so every bite has some snap.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Make garlic butter

    Mix the 2 tablespoons of softened butter, 2 cloves of minced garlic, and 3 tablespoons of grated parmesan until combined.

  2. 02

    Toast

    Spread the mixture thickly on 2 slices of crusty bread. Broil for 3 to 4 minutes until the edges are golden and the parmesan is crispy. Add fresh parsley and chilli flakes.

Easy swaps

No salted butter? Soft cheeses, mashed avocado, or ricotta play the same role, while garlic can keep the topping interesting. Whatever the spread, season it directly instead of salting only the top.

Storage

Toast cannot be stored assembled. Keep salted butter and garlic covered away from the bread, then toast a fresh slice and rebuild it when you are ready.

Slop Notes

Broiling rather than baking gives you the best crispy outside, soft inside texture.
