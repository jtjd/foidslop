Slop of the Day — June 11, 2026

GARLIC
PARMESAN TOAST.

Published June 11, 2026
Toast Comfort Quick
Garlic Parmesan Toast — foidslop

The simplest, most satisfying thing. Crispy garlic toast with melted parmesan.

At a Glance

Prep
3m
Cook
8m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

  • thick crusty bread 2 slices
  • salted butter, softened 2 tbsp
  • garlic, minced or pressed 2 cloves
  • parmesan, finely grated 3 tbsp
  • fresh parsley, chilli flakes optional

Method

  1. 01

    Make garlic butter

    Mix the 2 tablespoons of softened butter, 2 cloves of minced garlic, and 3 tablespoons of grated parmesan until combined.

  2. 02

    Toast

    Spread the mixture thickly on 2 slices of crusty bread. Broil for 3 to 4 minutes until the edges are golden and the parmesan is crispy. Add fresh parsley and chilli flakes.

Slop Notes

Broiling rather than baking gives you the best crispy outside, soft inside texture.
