SPAGHETTI SQUASH
WITH MARINARA.

Roasted spaghetti squash strands tossed with marinara and parmesan make a cozy vegetable-forward dinner.

At a Glance

Cook

Serves

Difficulty

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Roast squash

    Preheat oven to 400F/200C. Halve the squash and scoop out seeds. Rub with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Roast face down for 35-40 minutes until tender.

  2. 02

    Scrape

    Use a fork to scrape the flesh into spaghetti like strands.

  3. 03

    Sauce

    Toss the strands with warm marinara sauce. Top with parmesan, chilli flakes, and basil.

Slop Notes

Do not overcook the squash or the strands will become soft and lose their shape.
