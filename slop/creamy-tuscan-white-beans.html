Butter beans simmered in garlic, tomatoes, and spinach. Italian pantry perfection.

This version of Creamy Tuscan White Beans is scaled around one pan and one appetite. Give butter beans or cannellini enough time to brown or soften, then use garlic for contrast at the finish.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Build

    Saute 3 cloves of sliced garlic in 2 tablespoons of oil, add a handful of tomatoes until they burst. Add 1 can of beans and a splash of wine, simmering and mashing some beans to thicken.

  2. 02

    Add beans

    Stir in a handful of spinach until wilted. Season. Drizzle with more oil. Serve with bread and parmesan.

Easy swaps

Chickpeas or Great Northern beans can replace butter beans or cannellini. Kale works instead of spinach but needs a few extra minutes, and canned tomatoes can replace fresh cherry tomatoes.

Storage

Refrigerate leftovers covered for up to three days. Reheat gently until hot throughout, adding a splash of water if butter beans or cannellini or the sauce looks dry.

Slop Notes

Lightly mashing some of the beans is what makes the sauce creamy without adding any cream.
