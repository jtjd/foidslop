SOLO CHARCUTERIE BOARD
WITH WINE.

Published July 31, 2026
No CookCheeseFancy
Solo Charcuterie Board with Wine / foidslop
Home/Archive/Solo Charcuterie Board with Wine

A proper small board built for one, with wine. This is aspirational solo dining.

The arrangement is part of the recipe because it keeps every texture distinct. Put it on a proper plate, bring the sharp and creamy things together, and take your time.

At a Glance

Prep
10m
Cook
0m
Serves
1
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Method

  1. 01

    Build the board

    Place 40g of manchego and 40g of brie at opposite corners of a board. Fold 30g of prosciutto and 30g of salami and fill gaps with olives, fruit, and 1 tbsp of nuts. Add crackers and pour a glass of wine.

  2. 02

    Eat slowly

    Let the cheese lose its refrigerator chill, then graze slowly and change the combinations as you go. Keep the crackers dry and pour the wine only when the board is ready.

Slop Notes

Never rush a board. The whole point is that you eat slowly and enjoy it.
