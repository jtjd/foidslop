Sweet, chewy Hawaiian butter mochi cubes. A dense, satisfying treat.

Warm the butter mochi only enough to soften the center while keeping the edges pleasantly chewy. Serve the bites in a small pile with berries or ice cream alongside rather than underneath so the mochi does not turn wet.

    Serve

    If they are not fresh, microwave the mochi bites for 10 seconds to soften. Serve with berries or ice cream if desired.

Easy swaps

Any plain or coconut-forward butter mochi works here, whether homemade or store-bought. Fresh mango, pineapple, or strawberries can replace berries, and whipped cream can replace ice cream for a lighter finish.

Storage

Keep butter mochi covered at room temperature for a day or refrigerated for several days depending on the recipe or package. Rewarm only the portion you plan to eat so repeated heating does not toughen the rice flour.

Slop Notes

Butter mochi is made with coconut milk and glutinous rice flour. It is dense and incredibly buttery.
