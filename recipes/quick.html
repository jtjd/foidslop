A better starting point

How to make a quick dinner feel complete

  • Start with the ingredient that takes longest, even if that only means putting water on to boil.
  • Use one sharp or bright finish such as lemon, pickles, hot sauce, herbs, or a good shower of black pepper.
  • Keep a few fast foundations around: bread, eggs, noodles, canned beans, frozen rice, and something creamy.
