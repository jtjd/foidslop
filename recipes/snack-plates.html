A better starting point

Build a snack plate that eats like dinner

  • Pick one anchor such as cheese, tinned fish, hummus, eggs, or cured meat.
  • Add something crisp, something fresh, and something sharp or briny.
  • Bread and crackers are not decoration. Include enough to carry the rest of the plate.
