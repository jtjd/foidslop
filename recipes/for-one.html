A better starting point

Cooking for one without waste

  • Buy flexible ingredients that can move between toast, pasta, bowls, and snack plates.
  • Freeze bread, cooked grains, and extra portions of sauces before they become a problem.
  • Treat the serving size as a starting point. A bigger appetite can add bread, greens, or an egg.
