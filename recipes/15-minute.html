The OG Girl Dinner

A better starting point

Make the fifteen minutes count

  • Use pre-cooked grains, canned beans, quick noodles, and ingredients that are good straight from the package.
  • Prep while water boils or the pan heats, but do not rush the step that creates browning.
  • Keep the finish simple. One sauce, one herb, or one crunchy topping is enough.

Common questions

Does fifteen minutes include prep time?

Yes. Combined prep and cooking stays within fifteen minutes for every recipe in this collection.

What keeps these recipes fast?

Ready-to-eat foundations: pre-cooked grains, canned beans, quick-cooking noodles, and ingredients that are good straight from the package.

How do I keep fast food from tasting rushed?

Do not skip the browning step, season every layer, and finish with one bright element such as lemon, vinegar, or fresh herbs.
