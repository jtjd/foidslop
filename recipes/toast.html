A better starting point

The difference between toast and good toast

  • Use bread thick enough to stay crisp beneath the topping.
  • Season each layer, especially mild ingredients such as avocado, ricotta, mushrooms, and tomatoes.
  • Add the wettest toppings at the last minute so the bread keeps its crunch.

Common questions

When does toast count as dinner?

When the bread is thick and properly crisp, the topping brings real substance, and there is enough of it to satisfy you. Every recipe here follows those rules.

What bread works best?

A sturdy sourdough or country loaf. Thin sandwich bread goes soggy under anything wet.

Sweet or savory?

Both. Savory versions lean on eggs, fish, cheese, and vegetables, while sweet ones balance honey, fruit, or jam against salt and fat.
