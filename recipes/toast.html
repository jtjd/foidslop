The difference between toast and good toast

  • Use bread thick enough to stay crisp beneath the topping.
  • Season each layer, especially mild ingredients such as avocado, ricotta, mushrooms, and tomatoes.
  • Add the wettest toppings at the last minute so the bread keeps its crunch.
