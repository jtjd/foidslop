A better starting point

A useful no-cook formula

  • Choose a base such as yogurt, cottage cheese, hummus, tinned fish, or a good cheese.
  • Add produce for crunch and freshness, then something briny or acidic to wake everything up.
  • Finish with bread, crackers, pita, or chips so the plate has enough substance.

Common questions

Can you really make dinner without cooking?

Yes. These recipes assemble ready-to-eat ingredients such as tinned fish, cheese, bread, produce, and dips into a plate that eats like a real meal.

How do no-cook meals stay satisfying?

Contrast does the work: something creamy, something crisp, something briny or acidic, and enough bread or crackers to carry it all.

Are no-cook recipes right for hot days?

They are built for them. Nothing heats up the kitchen, and most come together in the time it takes to open a tin and slice something.
