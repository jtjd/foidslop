A better starting point

Better eggs for dinner

  • Cook eggs over gentler heat than feels right; they turn tough when rushed.
  • Something on the side matters: toast, crackers, or vegetables turn eggs into dinner.
  • Decide on the texture before the pan gets hot. Soft scrambled and jammy sit minutes apart.

Eggs for dinner questions

Is it weird to eat eggs at night?

Not here. Eggs cook in minutes, cost little, and take on almost any flavor you put next them, which is exactly what an easy dinner should do.

Are eggs filling enough for dinner?

With support, yes. Every recipe here pairs eggs with bread, potatoes, beans, cheese, or vegetables so the plate actually satisfies.

Which recipe is fastest?

Fried egg on toast with hot sauce is the sprinter: one pan, one slice of bread, and about five minutes from fridge to table.
