What is foidslop? Originally internet slang used to describe the heavily marketed, low-effort "healthy" meals pushed by social media influencers (think avocado toast, girl dinner, fruit protein smoothies, and cottage cheese bowls), the term has been reclaimed. foidslop is now a daily meal recipe site and merch brand. Every day we publish one slop of the day: a real, aesthetic recipe with full ingredients and instructions. Alongside the daily recipes, we drop the DJT Nippon collection: Japanese-inspired graphic tees, hoodies, posters, and mugs. Browse the recipe archive or shop the DJT Nippon line.
