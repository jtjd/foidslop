Slop Dictionary / slop

What Does Slop Mean Online?

The internet found one word for everything it resents and immediately overclocked it.

Short version

Online, slop is a flexible insult for low-quality, disposable, repetitive, mass-produced, or lazily assembled content and products. It can also be affectionate when the speaker enjoys the slop in question.

Why slop got huge

Merriam-Webster now includes low-quality AI-generated digital content as a definition of slop, alongside the older meanings involving waste, swill, and thin food.

The word works because it is physical. Slop is not merely bad. It sounds poured, shoveled, ladled, and produced in bulk. Perfect for an era where everyone feels like the feed is being filled faster than anyone can consume it.

The suffix era

Once slop became productive slang, compounds multiplied: AI slop, content slop, goyslop, foidslop, guyslop, moidslop. The prefix changes. The accusation stays roughly the same: this stuff was made to be consumed, not respected.

Can slop be affectionate?

Constantly. People call a beloved franchise slop while buying the collector edition. They call dinner slop while scraping the bowl clean. Contempt and affection now share custody of the word.

Related cursed vocabulary

AI slopWhat Is AI Slop?

AI slop is low-quality digital content produced in quantity with generative AI, especially content that is repetitive, disposable, inaccurate, or made mainly to fill feeds and search results.

foidslopWhat Is Foidslop?

Foidslop is internet slang for food, media, usernames, aesthetics, or other things perceived as strongly female-coded. The word started as an insult. It is also increasingly used as a joke, a label, and a reclaimed category by people who enjoy the exact things it is supposed to mock.

goyslopWhat Does Goyslop Mean?

Goyslop is offensive internet slang for low-quality food or media supposedly pushed on ordinary consumers. Its original usage is tied to antisemitic conspiracy theories about Jews controlling what non-Jews consume.

girl dinnerWhat Is Girl Dinner?

Girl dinner is a social-media term for an informal, often low-effort meal made from snacky or miscellaneous foods instead of a conventional plated dinner.
