Slop Dictionary / slop

What Does Slop Mean Online?

AI slop. Franchise slop. Foidslop. Reality slop. One ugly little word does a lot of work.

Short version

Slop is internet slang for cheap, repetitive, disposable, mass-produced, or low-effort content and products. It is also used affectionately for things the speaker fully intends to keep consuming.

Why is slop useful?

Slop says more than “bad.” It suggests bulk. Another image, another sequel, another post, another bowl, another episode is already coming down the pipe.

That makes it useful for AI content, franchise movies, reality television, frozen food, trend products, spammy posts, and anything else that feels produced by the bucket.

Slop can be good

People call things slop while actively enjoying them. Dating-show slop. Vampire slop. Seasonal coffee slop. Fast-food slop. This is normal usage now.

The word can mean trash, comfort food, genre product, guilty pleasure, or simply “there is a lot of this and I am still consuming it.”

Common slop compounds

AI slop, content slop, franchise slop, foidslop, goyslop, guyslop, moidslop. The prefix tells you what kind. Slop does the rest.

It is one of those internet words that doubles as a word-building kit.

Related vocabulary

AI slopWhat Is AI Slop?

AI slop means low-quality, repetitive, disposable, or mass-produced content made with generative AI, especially when it is produced at scale for search, feeds, or engagement.

foidslopWhat Is Foidslop?

Foidslop means female-coded slop. Food, media, games, usernames, aesthetics, music, books, and other things can all count. The word combines foid, 4chan/incel slang for a woman, with slop.

goyslopWhat Does Goyslop Mean?

Goyslop is far-right internet slang for mass-market food, media, or consumer products portrayed as cheap, homogenized, or made for passive consumption. The term often appears in antisemitic conspiracy posting.

girl dinnerWhat Is Girl Dinner?

Girl dinner is an informal meal made from snacky, leftover, or miscellaneous foods instead of a conventional plated dinner. The term went viral on TikTok in 2023.
