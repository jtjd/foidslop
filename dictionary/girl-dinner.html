Slop Dictionary / girl dinner

What Is Girl Dinner?

A meal assembled from fragments, then accidentally given a global brand strategy.

Short version

Girl dinner is a social-media term for an informal, often low-effort meal made from snacky or miscellaneous foods instead of a conventional plated dinner.

Where did girl dinner come from?

The phrase took off on TikTok in 2023 after Olivia Maher posted a plate of bread, cheese, grapes, and cornichons and compared it to something a medieval peasant might eat. The format spread almost immediately.

Soon girl dinner meant everything from a beautiful snack board to ramen, pickles, cereal, leftovers, or a collection of foods that only make sense to the person eating them.

Why did it stick?

Because it named a meal people were already eating. Cooking for yourself often looks different from cooking for a household. Sometimes dinner is an assembled plate, not a project with a cutting board and a sink full of evidence.

Girl dinner vs foidslop

Girl dinner is the mainstream cousin. Foidslop is sharper, more online, and less confined to food. A snack plate can be both. So can the general philosophy of feeding yourself without pretending a camera crew is coming over.

foidslopWhat Is Foidslop?

Foidslop is internet slang for food, media, usernames, aesthetics, or other things perceived as strongly female-coded. The word started as an insult. It is also increasingly used as a joke, a label, and a reclaimed category by people who enjoy the exact things it is supposed to mock.

slopWhat Does Slop Mean Online?

Online, slop is a flexible insult for low-quality, disposable, repetitive, mass-produced, or lazily assembled content and products. It can also be affectionate when the speaker enjoys the slop in question.
