A better starting point

Build a better tinned-fish dinner

  • Taste the fish before adding salt; tins packed in brine, oil, or sauce arrive with very different seasoning.
  • Use acid generously. Lemon, vinegar, pickles, capers, and hot sauce cut through oil and smoke.
  • Keep the base crisp by draining the fish lightly and assembling toast or crackers immediately before eating.
  • Treat the liquid in a good tin as an ingredient: spoon a little oil or escabeche sauce over the finished plate.

Tinned fish questions

What tinned fish is easiest to start with?

Tuna and smoked oysters are approachable, while sardines and anchovies bring a stronger savory flavor. Choose a tin packed in olive oil when you want the fish to carry the whole plate.

What should I serve with tinned fish?

Crisp toast or crackers, something acidic such as lemon or pickles, and a fresh element such as cucumber, herbs, apple, or tomato make a complete small meal.

How do I store an opened tin?

Move leftovers and some of their packing liquid to a covered container, refrigerate promptly, and use within two days. Do not store leftovers in the opened metal tin.
