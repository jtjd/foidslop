Overview

foidslop ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website foidslop.com. This page explains what information we collect when you visit, and how it is used. We keep it simple because our site is simple.

Information We Collect

Foidslop does not offer user accounts. We use the following services to operate the publication:

  • Weekly dispatch: Kit (opens in new tab) receives your email address when you subscribe. Kit processes double opt-in, delivery, opens and clicks where those measurements are enabled, unsubscribes, and suppression records used to prevent unwanted mail.
  • Community submissions: Tally (opens in new tab) receives your vote and, when you choose to provide them, a name or alias, written report, photo, and publication-permission choices.
  • Recipe ratings: when you rate a recipe, we store the star value and recipe name in our hosting provider's key-value storage. To prevent duplicate votes without accounts or tracking cookies, the request IP address is combined with a random salt and reduced to a one-way hash that cannot be reversed to identify you. Only the star rating, recipe, aggregate counts, and that hash are stored.
  • Google Analytics: we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site, including pages viewed, time on site, device and browser type, approximate location (city/region level), and referral source. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect this data. See the Google Privacy Policy (opens in new tab).
  • Cloudflare: our hosting and CDN provider may log IP addresses, browser type, and request metadata for security and performance purposes. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy (opens in new tab).

Email and Community Data

Newsletter subscriptions use double opt-in. You can unsubscribe through the link in any dispatch. Kit may keep delivery and suppression records after an unsubscribe so the address is not mailed again.

Community responses are used to count votes, guide editorial decisions, and consider reader reports for publication. GitHub Actions retrieves completed Tally submissions during the weekly count, calculates aggregate totals, and may move the winning recipe to Friday's publication date. Raw responses, names, comments, photos, upload links, and permission choices are not committed to the website repository or printed in automation logs.

A name, response, or photo is published only when the submission includes explicit publication permission. Tally submissions remain in our account until they are manually removed or deletion is requested. A permission record may be retained while the related reader material remains published.

We do not sell email addresses, community submissions, or analytics data.

Analytics

When you first visit foidslop, you'll see a banner asking whether to allow Google Analytics. We do not load Google Analytics or set any analytics cookies until you choose "Accept." If you choose "Decline," no analytics scripts run and no cookies are set for that purpose.

Once loaded, Google Analytics (gtag.js) collects aggregated, anonymized usage statistics such as pages viewed, time on site, device and browser type, approximate location, and referral source. This helps us understand which recipes and pages are popular. Google Analytics data is processed by Google and may be used in accordance with Google's own privacy policy and data practices.

To change your choice, clear your browser's local storage for this site, or use the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on (opens in new tab) for opt-out across all websites.

Cookies

This website uses local browser storage to remember your analytics consent choice.

Third-party services may set their own cookies in your browser:

  • Google Analytics sets cookies (such as _ga and _gid) to distinguish unique visitors and track usage patterns.
  • Cloudflare may set security cookies (such as __cf_bm) for bot management.

We do not use advertising cookies or sell any data collected through these cookies.

Third-Party Links

The site submits newsletter signups to Kit and links to a community form hosted by Tally. It may also link to cited sources or other external services. Once you leave foidslop.com, the destination's privacy policy and terms apply.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top.

Contact

For privacy questions, corrections, or deletion requests, email dispatch@foidslop.com or reply to any dispatch. To stop receiving the newsletter, use the unsubscribe link in any email.
